What is Zush AI File Renamer?

Zush is an AI file renamer for Mac and Windows. It names files in 107 formats from their content, metadata, and your rules — review, batch rename, or undo anytime. Templates and folder monitoring automate repeat work, on Cloud AI or Local AI.

How Zush’s AI file renamer and organizer works

Zush reads the content and metadata of PDFs, photos, video and audio to suggest descriptive names. You review before applying, files stay put unless you turn on AI folder sorting (beta), and every rename or move can be undone.

Your naming system, powered by AI

Choose where AI runs

Choose Zush Cloud, BYOK, LM Studio or Ollama for the same review-first rename workflow.

Cloud AI

Goes through our relay to a commercial AI provider, under their standard API terms. The relay exists so the API key can’t be pulled out of the app.

Bring Your Own Key

Connect your own API key from Gemini, OpenRouter, OpenAI, or Claude for cloud renames; FREE includes 50 across all modes and PRO is unlimited. Keys are stored locally in secure platform storage.

Private Local AI Options

Keep supported analysis on your device with LM Studio or Ollama. Each local mode works offline after setup.
