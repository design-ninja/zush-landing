## Why people look past RenameClick

RenameClick is a capable app, now at rename.click. Version 4.1.2 (November 10, 2026) added video: MP4, MOV, M4V and 3GP files are named from frames plus local speech transcription. Version 4.0.1 added an MCP server for AI agents. The free plan gives unlimited analysis and 100 renames or moves a month; Pro is $8 plus VAT a month or $36 plus VAT lifetime.

The reasons to switch are now narrower and more specific:

- **MKV, WebM and AVI** are still unsupported, and camera RAW and design files are not on its format list.
- **Undo in the Rename workspace** holds 10 entries in memory and clears on restart. Auto Flow keeps eligible records.
- **The free AI path is a ~4 GB local model download**, with 16 GB of RAM recommended. Cloud models need your own key and provider bill.
- **Auto Flow runs only while the app is open.**

If none of those affect you, RenameClick may already be the right tool. It is also the easier choice if you want offline renaming without installing Ollama.

## 1. Zush: broader formats, persistent undo, cloud included

[Zush](/) reads each file and proposes a descriptive name, then waits for you to click Rename. It handles 100+ formats: photos including RAW and HEIC, PDFs, Office and iWork documents, PSD, Figma, Sketch and Illustrator files, audio, and video including MP4, MOV, MKV, AVI and WMV. Video names come from frames, speech, embedded subtitles and metadata.

What it changes compared with RenameClick:

- **Works right away.** 50 free AI renames on managed Zush Cloud AI, with no account, card, API key or model download. In cloud mode it needs 4 GB of RAM.
- **A license that includes the cloud.** $48 once covers Zush Cloud AI, BYOK (Gemini, OpenRouter, OpenAI, Claude), Ollama and LM Studio, all future updates, up to 3 devices across Mac and Windows, and a 14-day refund.
- **Undo that persists.** Rename History keeps every batch on disk. Undo All lists conflicts and can restore under a new name.
- **Duplicates in the same pass.** AI Rename can detect copies and keep them, mark them in the name, or move them to the Trash. Copies use no credits, and Undo brings them back.
- **Folder sorting by your own rules.** Write a rule such as "Group invoices by company and receipts by month", or use Auto. Monitor can apply it to new files, including on external drives on Mac. See [AI folder sorting](/docs/folder-sorting).

Where RenameClick is stronger: it has a dedicated AI Search workspace, an MCP server, and a bundled offline model. Zush has no content-search workspace or MCP server, and local AI needs Ollama or LM Studio.

Ratings as of November 17, 2026: Zush has 5.0 from 8 ratings on the Microsoft Store (US) and a 4.5/5 Softpedia editor score. RenameClick has 3.0 from 6 ratings and 3.5/5.

## 2. FilesDesk: credits, or bring your own key

FilesDesk charges 1 credit per AI name suggestion, and re-running a file uses another credit: $8 a month for 500 or $20 for 2,000, or $80 and $200 a year. The free tier has 15 credits and needs a sign-in. The $40 Self-Managed license is unmetered with your own Claude, OpenAI, Gemini or OpenRouter key, or with Ollama, LM Studio or vLLM. It binds to one device, includes 1 year of updates, and has a 3-day refund window.

It names images, documents, video (including MKV and WebM) and audio, and every operation lands in Processing History with one-click undo and redo. Watch Folders handle new files, and Watch Inbox renames email attachments.

Choose FilesDesk if you already pay for an AI provider and rename on one computer. Its weak point is that managed AI is subscription-only. See [Zush vs FilesDesk](/blog/zush-vs-filesdesk).

## 3. NameQuick: rules after renaming

NameQuick adds Rules that move or tag files once they are named, which suits recurring PDFs, receipts and contracts. It runs on Mac and Windows, and is also on the Mac App Store and Setapp.

Managed plans start at $12 a month for 500 renames. Rename packs cost $19 for 500 or $39 for 1,500, valid 12 months. You get 30 free Managed renames with no email or card; the 14-day Managed trial with 70 renames needs a card. The $69 Self-Managed license covers one computer with lifetime updates, using your own key or Ollama, LM Studio or MLX.

Its weak point: History and Undo exist, but undo can fail if a file has since moved. Its site does not state video, RAW or design support. See [Zush vs NameQuick](/blog/zush-vs-namequick).

## 4. Renamer.ai: document-first, desktop and web

Renamer.ai focuses on documents and scans, with a desktop app and a web interface. Local mode is available on every tier, including the free Starter plan with 25 files a month. Paid plans are $9.95 (200 files), $29.95 (1,000) and $99.95 (5,000) per month, with 15% off annual billing. Bring-your-own-key is only on the $99.95 Ultimate plan.

It lists HEIC, Office, EPS, SVG and AI files, but not camera RAW, PSD or video. Magic Folders and undo are included. Its Softpedia editor score is 4.5/5, the same as Zush.

Choose it if you want a web app with no install. See [Renamer.ai alternatives](/blog/renamer-ai-alternatives).

## 5. AI Renamer: cheapest one-time license, and Linux

AI Renamer costs $19 once for Local mode (your own key or a local model) or $10 per 200 cloud credits, with 10 free credits. You sign in with a Google account. It connects to Ollama, LM Studio, OpenAI, Google AI Studio, OpenRouter or a custom endpoint, and it is the only app here with Linux builds.

It moved fast in November 2026. Version 1.9.0 added a Watch Folder beta that runs only while the app is open and skips subfolders. Version 1.10.0 added video, audio, RAW, HEIC, Office and EPUB support, Name Templates, and Intel Mac and Linux arm64 builds. It shows a History list, but undo is not documented.

Its weak point is recovery: test on copies. See [Zush vs AI Renamer](/blog/zush-vs-airenamer).

## FAQ

### What is the best RenameClick alternative?

For most people, Zush. It runs on Mac and Windows, adds MKV video, RAW and design files, keeps a persistent Rename History with batch undo, and its $48 one-time license includes managed cloud AI on up to 3 devices. Pick FilesDesk if you bring your own key, NameQuick for rule-based document filing, Renamer.ai for a web app, or AI Renamer for Linux.

### Does RenameClick support video now?

Yes. Version 4.1.2 (November 10, 2026) names MP4, MOV, M4V and 3GP videos from sampled frames plus local speech transcription. MKV, WebM and AVI are still listed as unsupported. Among the alternatives here, Zush and FilesDesk both list MKV, and AI Renamer added video in version 1.10.0 without publishing a container list.

### Is RenameClick free?

Partly. The free plan includes unlimited analysis and 100 renames or moves a month, but the free AI path means downloading a ~4 GB local model, with 16 GB of RAM recommended, and going online once before the first apply. Zush gives 50 AI renames in total on its managed cloud, with no account, card or download.

### Which RenameClick alternative is the best value for a one-time price?

It depends on what you want included. AI Renamer ($19), FilesDesk ($40) and NameQuick ($69) one-time licenses run on your own API key or a local model, so cloud AI costs extra. Zush's $48 license includes managed cloud AI, BYOK and local models, all future updates and 3 devices across Mac and Windows.

### Which RenameClick alternative keeps files on my computer?

Zush works with Ollama or LM Studio, so analysis stays on your Mac or PC; Zush Cloud AI and BYOK send the content needed for naming to a cloud model. FilesDesk and NameQuick run local models on their self-managed licenses, Renamer.ai offers local mode on every tier, and AI Renamer's $19 Local license works with Ollama or LM Studio.

### How do I switch from RenameClick to Zush?

Start with the files RenameClick doesn't cover: MKV videos, RAW photos, design files and iWork documents. Zush's 50 free renames need no account, so you can test them on copies right away. If you used your own API key in RenameClick, add the same key in Zush's BYOK settings, and provider costs stay with your provider. If you ran Ollama or LM Studio, select the same runtime in Zush.
