## Where RenameClick falls short

[RenameClick](https://rename.click/) renames images, documents, and audio (with local speech transcription), and its Auto Flow can rename and move new files. The free plan has unlimited analysis and 100 successful renames or moves a month. Pro is $8 plus VAT per month or $36 plus VAT lifetime; verify the live checkout.

Common reasons to look elsewhere:

- **Video is not supported**, and RAW photos and design files are not in its published format list.
- **Recovery differs by workspace.** Standard Rename keeps up to 10 undo entries in memory and clears them on restart; Auto Flow saves eligible applied records and supports Undo/Redo across restarts.
- **The local model is a multi-gigabyte download** that needs spare memory to run well.
- **Included cloud AI differs.** RenameClick’s optional cloud providers use your key and bill separately; Zush PRO includes managed cloud AI. Both apps now have visual naming editors.

RenameClick 3.1 adds a visual format editor with preview, while current sorting supports custom folder sets and existing nested folders. A missing pattern builder or fixed-only category list is no longer a reason to switch. See its [format editor](https://rename.click/docs/customization/format-patterns) and [sorting documentation](https://rename.click/docs/customization/categories).

## 1. Zush: broader formats and persistent history

[Zush](/) reads each file and proposes a descriptive name, then waits for you to approve the batch. It handles 100+ formats: photos including RAW and HEIC, PDFs and Office and iWork documents, PSD, Figma, Sketch and Illustrator files, 16 video formats, and audio.

The main differences from RenameClick:

- **Rename History that persists.** Zush retains original and new names, folders, and timestamps. Undo All reports restored files and problems, with restoration under a new name when the original location is occupied.
- **A pattern you build.** Templates and 145+ Naming Blocks set field order and formatting. Sequence Number adds your starting number and leading zeros, continuing separately in each folder. Custom AI Blocks cover fields specific to your work.
- **Choice of AI.** Zush Cloud AI works without setup. BYOK connects your own Gemini, OpenRouter, OpenAI, or Claude key. Ollama or LM Studio keep analysis on your machine.
- **Optional AI folder sorting.** Choose Auto grouping or write Folder rules such as “Group invoices by company and receipts by month.” Select a Destination folder and optionally Reuse existing folders; review names and folders before applying. Leave sorting off and keep the original destination to rename in place.

Save the grouping settings in a Template and assign it to Monitor for new files directly inside the watched folder. Changing the Monitor Template does not automatically reorganize already processed files. See [AI folder sorting](/docs/folder-sorting).

Zush also supports nested folder rules, such as grouping by year and then month. You can sort already renamed files without changing their names again or spending another rename credit. Review the destinations before applying. This separate action handles an existing batch; changing a Monitor Template does not retroactively reorganize it. [AI folder sorting](/docs/folder-sorting) works on Mac and Windows.

RenameClick has a dedicated AI Search workspace for finding files by content; Zush relies on searchable names plus Finder tags and Spotlight metadata instead. Full comparison: [Zush vs RenameClick](/blog/zush-vs-renameclick).

## 2. FilesDesk: credits or bring your own key

[FilesDesk](https://filesdesk.app/) charges 1 credit per filename suggestion: $8 a month for 500 or $20 for 2,000. The $40 lifetime plan is unmetered but needs your own API key or a local model. It supports images, documents, video, and audio, with Watch Folders and undo through Processing History.

Good if you already pay for an AI provider. See [Zush vs FilesDesk](/blog/zush-vs-filesdesk).

## 3. NameQuick: rules after renaming

[NameQuick](https://www.namequick.app/) adds conditional Rules that move, tag, or archive files once they are renamed. Managed plans start at $12 a month and meter renames; the $69 Self-Managed license is unmetered with your own key or local models.

Consider it for conditional document workflows on Mac or Windows; Finder actions and MLX are Mac-only. See [Zush vs NameQuick](/blog/zush-vs-namequick).

## 4. Renamer.ai: documents, web access, and local options

**Renamer.ai release rechecked October 25, 2026.** [Renamer.ai](https://renamer.ai/) focuses on OCR-heavy documents and has desktop and web interfaces. Its [v5 releases](https://renamer.ai/changelog) add local models, OpenAI/Gemini BYOK, and reusable folder structures; 5.0.4 adds bulk retries and external-drive recovery for Magic Folders. The published cloud plans have monthly allowances of 200, 1,000, or 5,000 files; separate Local/BYOK license limits need confirmation. Its public list does not document camera RAW, PSD, Sketch, or Figma. See [Renamer.ai alternatives](/blog/renamer-ai-alternatives). Unchanged vendor details retain their earlier checks.

## 5. AI Renamer: simple and cheap

[AI Renamer](https://airenamer.app/) is a desktop app with a $19 one-time local mode or cloud credits at $10 per 200. It keeps things simple: custom prompts, common images and text documents. Folder monitoring and rename history are not documented. See [Zush vs AI Renamer](/blog/zush-vs-airenamer).

## How to choose

- You rename video, RAW, or design files: **Zush**.
- You want persistent history for every manual rename batch, not only eligible Auto Flow records: **Zush**.
- You want BYOK and included managed cloud on the same license: **Zush**. Compare **FilesDesk** if its conditional Organize Flow or vLLM support is required.
- You need conditional document actions beyond folder grouping: **NameQuick**, with platform-specific limits.
- You mainly rename scanned documents and prefer a web app: **Renamer.ai**.
- One-time purchase, basic needs: **AI Renamer**.

## FAQ

### Can Zush sort files into folders as well as rename them?

Yes. Its optional AI folder sorting works on Mac and Windows. Choose Auto or write Folder rules, select the destination, and review both names and groups in a manual batch. A sorting-enabled Template can also process new files directly inside a monitored folder; changing its Template does not automatically reorganize files already processed. Leave sorting off and keep the original destination for naming only.

### What is the best RenameClick alternative?

Zush is our pick for mixed-format Mac and Windows archives: $48 one-time with managed cloud AI, documented RAW/design/video support, per-folder numbering, and persistent recovery with conflict handling. RenameClick also has a visual editor, so that feature alone is not a reason to switch.

### Is RenameClick free?

Partly. RenameClick's free plan currently includes unlimited analysis and 100 successful renames or moves per month. Zush includes 50 AI renames in total across all modes, with no monthly reset and no credit card.

### Which RenameClick alternative works offline?

Zush works offline with Ollama or LM Studio on Mac and Windows. FilesDesk and NameQuick also support local models on their self-managed plans.

### Can I use RenameClick and Zush together?

Yes, though most people pick one. Both rename by content and can sort files into folders. RenameClick adds a dedicated AI Search workspace; Zush combines optional folder sorting, broader formats, persistent history, and structured filename control. Test the grouping settings on copies before automating either tool.
