## Zush

![Zush AI rename results showing files with descriptive before and after names and green checkmarks](/images/showcase/macos/batch-rename-light.webp)

Zush is a desktop AI file renamer for mixed folders. It reads each file, proposes a name, and changes nothing until you click Rename. You can double-click any suggestion to edit it first. Names are built from ready-made Templates (Movies & TV, Remove Duplicates and others), 145+ Naming Blocks, Custom AI Blocks, and settings for case, separators, dates and language. Rename History keeps the original name, new name, folder and time of every batch, so you can undo a batch next week.

A folder with `IMG_4821.HEIC`, `_DSC0019.NEF`, `checkout-flow.fig`, `voice-memo.m4a`, `client-brief.pages`, `episode-03.mkv` and `invoice.pdf` stays in one workflow.

The free tier gives 50 AI renames on Zush Cloud AI with no account, card, API key or model download. PRO is $10 a month or $48 once. Both remove the limit across Zush Cloud AI, BYOK (Gemini, OpenRouter, OpenAI, Claude), LM Studio and Ollama. One license covers up to 3 devices on Mac and Windows, and you can [export Templates](/docs/template-import-export) to move your setup between them.

## RenameClick

![RenameClick desktop app interface (image: rename.click)](/images/blog/competitors/renameclick.webp)

RenameClick is a local-first desktop app for macOS and Windows, now at rename.click. Its built-in provider runs a local model of about 4 GB on your device without an API key; the vendor recommends 16 GB of RAM. It also connects to LM Studio, Ollama, and OpenAI-compatible APIs such as OpenRouter in its direct Mac and Windows builds (not the Mac App Store build), with provider usage billed separately.

Two recent releases changed the picture. Version 4.0.1 added an MCP server, so AI agents can drive RenameClick. Version 4.1.2 (November 10, 2026) added video: MP4, MOV, M4V and 3GP files are named from sampled frames plus local speech transcription. MKV, WebM and AVI are still listed as unsupported.

RenameClick is closer to a file organizer than a pure renamer. It renames, moves files into folder sets, runs Auto Flow watch rules, and has an AI Search workspace that finds files by meaning, so a search for invoices can match a scan called `scan_001.pdf`.

## Pricing: what each license includes

Zush costs $48 once or $10 a month, which adds up to $120 for one year, $360 for three years, or $600 for five years before taxes. RenameClick costs $36 plus VAT once or $8 plus VAT a month. The useful comparison is what the one-time price buys:

- **Zush $48:** unlimited renames on managed Zush Cloud AI as well as BYOK and local models, all future updates, up to 3 devices across Mac and Windows, and a 14-day refund window.
- **RenameClick $36 + VAT:** unlimited renames with the built-in local model, Ollama, LM Studio, or your own API key. Cloud provider fees are yours. Lifetime updates, up to 3 devices on direct builds, and refunds only where the law requires them.

If you want cloud-quality names without running a model or paying a provider, Zush's license covers that and RenameClick's does not. If you want everything on-device with no extra setup, RenameClick's bundled model is the simpler route; Zush needs Ollama or LM Studio for that.

Free allowances also differ. Zush gives 50 renames in total with no monthly reset. RenameClick gives 100 renames or moves every month, after the model download.

## Video and media

Both apps now name video from what is in it. The differences are in coverage and evidence:

- **Containers.** Zush reads MP4, MOV, MKV, AVI, WMV and other common formats. RenameClick reads MP4, MOV, M4V and 3GP and lists MKV, WebM and AVI as unsupported. If your library comes from downloads or screen recorders that write MKV, this matters.
- **Evidence.** Zush combines sampled frames, speech, embedded subtitle tracks (including inside MKV files) and file metadata. RenameClick combines frames with local speech transcription.
- **Local speech.** Zush can transcribe locally through Ollama or LM Studio (Mac 3.20.1); RenameClick does it with its own local stack.
- **TV and film.** Zush's Movies & TV template names episodes by show, season and episode number and can sort them into show and season folders.

On Windows, Zush can also identify music that has no tags. That feature is not on Mac yet. See [audio and video analysis](/docs/audio-video) and [rename videos with AI](/rename-videos-with-ai).

## Photos, design files and documents

RenameClick covers common images and a long document list well, with text extraction for PDFs and Office files and vision fallback for scans. Its docs do not list camera RAW, Sketch, Figma or PSD files.

Zush lists 11 RAW variants (CR2, CR3, NEF, ARW, DNG, ORF, RAF, RW2, PEF, SRW, SR2), design files (Sketch, Figma, Illustrator, Photoshop), iWork documents, EPUB and subtitles. If those formats are why you are reading this, test them first.

## Undo and recovery

This is the largest practical gap. RenameClick's standard Rename workspace keeps up to 10 undo entries in memory and clears them when the app restarts. Its Auto Flow keeps eligible records across restarts.

Zush keeps a persistent [Rename History](/docs/undo-history) for every batch. Undo All restores the batch, lists any conflicts (for example, a new file now using the old name), and offers to restore under a new name. Duplicate copies that Zush moved to the Trash during AI Rename are restored by Undo as well.

## Automation and sorting

Both apps watch folders and move files.

Zush's Monitor renames new files with a Template and can sort them by plain-language Folder rules or Auto, including nested folders. Rules can also use file details such as dates and page counts (the Mac app also uses size, camera and duration). You can sort already renamed files without renaming them again. On Mac, monitoring covers external drives and resumes after you reconnect one. See the [folder sorting guide](/docs/folder-sorting).

RenameClick's Auto Flow is a set of rule rows, each with a source folder, destination, naming format, instructions, language and sorting preset. It waits for files to finish writing, avoids loops inside destination folders, and pauses when free-plan credits run out. It runs only while the app is open.

## Which one should you pick?

**Choose Zush if:**

- Your folders include MKV video, RAW photos, design files or iWork documents
- You want any batch to stay reversible after a restart
- You want a one-time price that includes managed cloud AI, with no model download or provider bill
- You want one license across Mac and Windows machines, up to 3 devices
- You want duplicates handled in the same pass as renaming

**Choose RenameClick if:**

- You want offline renaming with a bundled model and no Ollama setup, and your Mac or PC has the memory for it
- You need to search files by content in a dedicated workspace
- You want AI agents to run renames through an MCP server
- You prefer picking from folder sets over writing folder rules

Before buying either, copy ten representative files into a test folder and run both free tiers. Compare names, preview, unsupported formats, and whether you can reverse the whole batch.

## FAQ

### When should I choose Zush over RenameClick?

Choose Zush when a folder mixes RAW photos, design files, iWork documents, audio and MKV video, and you want names to follow a reusable Template. Zush previews every name, keeps a persistent Rename History with batch undo, and its $48 license includes managed cloud AI on up to 3 devices. RenameClick fits better if you need its bundled local model, AI Search, or MCP server.

### Does RenameClick support video files?

Yes, since version 4.1.2 (November 10, 2026). It names MP4, MOV, M4V and 3GP videos from sampled frames plus local speech transcription, and audio files such as MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A and AAC. MKV, WebM and AVI are still listed as unsupported. Zush handles MP4, MOV, MKV, AVI, WMV and more, and also reads embedded subtitle tracks. See the [supported format list](/docs/supported-file-types).

### Is RenameClick cheaper than Zush?

The sticker price is lower: $36 plus VAT once versus $48. The licenses cover different things. Zush's $48 includes managed Zush Cloud AI, BYOK and local models, all future updates, 3 devices across Mac and Windows, and a 14-day refund. RenameClick's lifetime license covers its local model or your own key, with provider fees on you and refunds only where the law requires them.

### Where are my files processed in each app?

RenameClick's built-in model runs on your device after a ~4 GB download; cloud providers you connect receive the content they need. In Zush, Ollama and LM Studio keep analysis on your computer, while Zush Cloud AI and BYOK send the content needed for naming to a cloud model. You pick the mode per Template, so private folders can stay local.

### Can I undo a batch rename after restarting?

In Zush, yes. Rename History is stored on disk and Undo All restores a whole batch, listing any name conflicts. In RenameClick's Rename workspace, undo keeps up to 10 entries in memory and clears them on restart; Auto Flow keeps eligible records across restarts. Test recovery on copies before running either app on a large folder.

### Does RenameClick support OpenRouter?

Yes, in its direct Mac and Windows builds, alongside other OpenAI-compatible APIs. The Mac App Store build does not include it, and the connected provider bills usage separately. Zush supports OpenRouter through BYOK on every plan, so OpenRouter alone is not a reason to switch either way.

### Does RenameClick support RAW photos?

Its published supported-files list does not include camera RAW formats such as CR2, CR3, NEF, ARW or DNG, or design files such as PSD, Sketch and Figma. Zush lists 11 RAW variants plus those design formats. If RAW files are part of your workflow, run a few through both free tiers before buying.

## Verdict

The overlap is now large: both run on Mac and Windows, rename by content, handle common video and audio, watch folders, support local AI, and sell a lifetime license.
