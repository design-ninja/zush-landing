Quick answer: pick **Wisfile** if you have a folder of PDFs or Word documents, want them renamed once, and do not want to pay. Pick [Zush](/) if your folders also hold screenshots, photos, scans, spreadsheets, or video, if new files keep arriving, or if you need to undo a batch later. Both run on Mac and Windows and both can keep processing on your computer.

## Where Wisfile is stronger

**It is free.** Every feature, no sign-up, no monthly cap. Zush gives 50 free renames and then needs a license.

**It is local with zero setup.** Wisfile ships its own engine, so nothing to install or configure. Zush can run locally too, but you first install [Ollama or LM Studio](/offline-ai-file-renamer) and pick a model; otherwise it uses a cloud route.

**It is tuned for academic papers.** Its examples center on extracting title, authors, and year from research PDFs and restoring proper paper titles. If that is your whole job, it is a focused fit.

## Where Zush is stronger

**It handles 100+ supported formats.** Wisfile's FAQ names Word and PDF. Zush also reads screenshots, phone photos including RAW and HEIC, image-only scans, Excel and PowerPoint, Pages and Numbers, design files, 16 video formats, and audio. One mixed Downloads folder can go through one batch.

![Zush for Mac showing each proposed filename for a mixed folder before the batch is applied](/images/showcase/macos/batch-rename-light.webp)

**It keeps working after the first cleanup.** Wisfile runs when you drag files in. Zush's **Monitor** watches Downloads, Desktop, or a scanner folder and names new files as they arrive, using the Template you assign. Sorting rules saved in the Template apply too.

**You can undo.** Wisfile's FAQ says renames cannot be undone and recommends testing on a small batch. Zush records every original and new name in Rename History, so you can inspect and revert an applied batch.

![Zush Activity tab listing each original and new filename with a button to revert the rename](/images/showcase/macos/activity-light.webp)

**You control the filename structure.** Ready-made Templates such as Bookkeeper Expenses, Screenshots, or Photographer Shoots combine with 145+ Naming Blocks: date, vendor, amount, client, capture date, and more. You set field order, case, separators, date format, and output language.

**You choose the AI.** Use the managed Zush Cloud AI, your own provider key, or a local model. Wisfile's engine is fixed.

One Zush license covers up to 3 devices on Mac and Windows.

You can [export Templates](/docs/template-import-export) with Naming Blocks, Custom AI Blocks, and folder rules, then import them on another computer. Choose local destinations, folder permissions, and AI connections again there; this transfers the workflow settings, not your files.

Zush also supports nested folder rules, such as grouping by year and then month. You can sort already renamed files without changing their names again or spending another rename credit. Review the destinations before applying. This separate action handles an existing batch; changing a Monitor Template does not retroactively reorganize it. [AI folder sorting](/docs/folder-sorting) works on Mac and Windows.

Zush’s [Sequence Number block](/docs/naming-blocks#add-sequence-numbers) adds a starting number and leading zeros, with numbering continuing separately in each folder. For a client archive, that means each destination can keep its own numbered series while the other blocks preserve the document date, client, and title.

[Undo All](/docs/undo-history#resolve-a-conflict) now separates restored files from files needing attention. If the original location is occupied, Zush offers restoration under a new name. That is useful when a batch has already been followed by new downloads or edits; review any unresolved entries rather than assuming every file was restored.

## Folder sorting: both do it, differently

Wisfile's AI Foldering detects a subject, field, or journal and groups similar files into shared folders. It is a quick one-click pass.

Zush plans names and folders in the same reviewed batch. Leave **Folder rules** empty for Auto grouping, or write an instruction of up to 2,000 characters, such as "Group invoices by company and receipts by month." With **Reuse existing folders** on, files go into folders you already have, and a new folder is created only when nothing fits. Review destinations before you apply.

## Same folder in both apps

Consider this illustrative workload, not a measured test: 30 PDFs, 20 screenshots, a few phone photos, two spreadsheets, and a screen recording.

Actual suggestions depend on the files and selected model.

If the folder were 50 research papers and nothing else, Wisfile would cover it at no cost.

## Which should you choose?

- One-time cleanup of research papers or Word documents, zero budget: **Wisfile**.
- Mixed folders with images, scans, spreadsheets, or video: **Zush**.
- Files keep arriving and should be named automatically: **Zush** with Monitor.
- You need a safety net to revert renames: **Zush**.
- You want local AI without installing anything else: **Wisfile**.

## FAQ

### Is Wisfile really free?

Yes. Its FAQ states that all features are free, and it requires no sign-up.

### Does Wisfile work offline?

Yes. Wisfile describes its processing as fully local, with no data leaving your device. Zush works offline too when you choose Ollama or LM Studio.

### Can Wisfile rename images or screenshots?

Its FAQ lists Word and PDF documents as supported for recognition and renaming. It does not describe image, screenshot, or video naming. Zush supports all of these.

### Can I undo renames in Wisfile?

Not with a built-in feature, according to its FAQ. Test on copies first. In Zush, Rename History lets you revert an applied batch.

### Is Zush or Wisfile better for invoices and receipts?

Zush, if you want a consistent pattern such as `date_vendor_amount` and automatic naming for new files. Its Bookkeeper Expenses Template is built for that. See [how to organize invoices and receipts](/blog/how-to-organize-invoices-and-receipts).
