Quick answer: pick **Wisfile** if you have a folder of PDFs or Word documents, want them renamed once, and don't want to pay. Pick [Zush](/) if your folders also hold screenshots, photos, scans, spreadsheets, or video, if new files keep arriving, or if you need to undo a batch later. Wisfile has no undo; Zush keeps a persistent Rename History. Both run on Mac and Windows, and both can keep processing on your computer.

**How we checked:** Written by the maker of [Zush](/). Competitor prices, features and ratings were checked on vendor sites and official stores on November 17, 2026 ([sources below](#sources-and-how-we-checked)). This compares documented features, not a lab test.

## Where Wisfile is stronger

**It is free.** Every feature, no sign-up, no monthly cap. For a one-off cleanup of PDFs and Word files, that is hard to beat. Zush's free tier is 50 renames; after that, the $48 license buys the broader format coverage, monitoring, and undo described below.

**It is local with zero setup.** Wisfile ships its own engine, so nothing to install or configure. Zush can run locally too, but you first install [Ollama or LM Studio](/offline-ai-file-renamer) and pick a model; otherwise it uses a cloud route.

**It is tuned for academic papers.** Its examples center on extracting title, authors, and year from research PDFs and restoring proper paper titles. If that is your whole job, it is a focused fit.

## Where Zush is stronger

**It handles 100+ supported formats.** Wisfile's FAQ names Word and PDF. Zush also reads screenshots, phone photos including RAW and HEIC, image-only scans, Excel and PowerPoint, Pages and Numbers, EPUB, design files, audio, and video. Videos, including MKV, are named from speech, embedded subtitles, and metadata. Duplicate copies are detected during the rename and use no credits. One mixed Downloads folder can go through one batch.

![Zush for Mac showing each proposed filename for a mixed folder before the batch is applied](/images/showcase/macos/batch-rename-light.webp)

**It keeps working after the first cleanup.** Wisfile runs when you drag files in. Zush's **Monitor** watches Downloads, Desktop, or a scanner folder and names new files as they arrive, using the Template you assign. Sorting rules saved in the Template apply too. On Mac, Monitor also covers external drives and resumes after you reconnect them.

**You can undo.** Wisfile's FAQ says renames cannot be undone and recommends testing on a small batch. Zush shows every proposed name first, and nothing changes until you click Rename. It then records every original and new name in a persistent Rename History, so you can revert an applied batch later. Undo All lists any conflicts and offers to restore under a new name.

![Zush Activity tab listing each original and new filename with a button to revert the rename](/images/showcase/macos/activity-light.webp)

**You control the filename structure.** Ready-made Templates such as Bookkeeper Expenses, Screenshots, or Photographer Shoots combine with 145+ Naming Blocks: date, vendor, amount, client, capture date, and more. You set field order, case, separators, date format, and output language.

**You choose the AI, and it works right away.** The first 50 renames run on managed Zush Cloud AI with no account, card, API key, or model download. You can also use your own provider key or a local model. Wisfile's engine is fixed.

## Folder sorting: both do it, differently

Wisfile's AI Foldering detects a subject, field, or journal and groups similar files into shared folders. It is a quick one-click pass.

Zush plans names and folders in the same reviewed batch. Leave **Folder rules** empty for Auto grouping, or write an instruction of up to 2,000 characters, such as "Group invoices by company and receipts by month." With **Reuse existing folders** on, files go into folders you already have, and a new folder is created only when nothing fits. Review destinations before you apply.

## Same folder in both apps

Take a typical work folder: 30 PDFs, 20 screenshots, a few phone photos, two spreadsheets, and a screen recording.

- **Wisfile** renames the PDFs and Word files from their content. Screenshots, photos, spreadsheets, and the video stay as they were, based on its published format list.
- **Zush** proposes a name for every file, for example `2026-09-12_acme_invoice_1042.pdf`, `stripe-revenue-dashboard-september.png`, or `q3-onboarding-demo-recording.mp4`, and can sort the batch into folders in the same step.

If the folder were 50 research papers and nothing else, Wisfile would cover it at no cost.

## FAQ

### Is Wisfile really free?

Yes. Its FAQ states that all features are free, and it requires no sign-up. That makes it a good first try for a one-off cleanup of PDFs and Word documents. Zush gives 50 free AI renames with no account, then costs $10 a month or $48 one-time, which adds 100+ formats, folder monitoring, and batch undo.

### Is Zush worth paying for when Wisfile is free?

It depends on your folders. If they hold only PDFs and Word files and you rename them once, Wisfile covers that at no cost. If they mix screenshots, photos, scans, spreadsheets, or video, or new files keep arriving, Zush's $48 one-time license covers all of that with managed cloud AI included, on up to 3 devices, with a 14-day refund.

### Does Wisfile work offline?

Yes. Wisfile describes its processing as fully local, with no data leaving your device, and it needs no model setup. Zush works offline too when you choose Ollama or LM Studio, but you install a model first. Its default Zush Cloud AI mode sends file content to the cloud for naming, and BYOK sends it to the provider you choose.

### Can Wisfile rename images or screenshots?

Its FAQ lists Word and PDF documents as supported for recognition and renaming. It does not describe image, screenshot, or video naming. Zush names screenshots, phone photos including RAW and HEIC, scans, design files, audio, and video from speech and subtitles. If your folders are mixed, test a few non-PDF files in each app before deciding.

### Can I undo renames in Wisfile?

Not with a built-in feature, according to its FAQ, which recommends testing on a small batch first. Work on copies if you use it. In Zush, you preview every name before anything changes, and the persistent Rename History lets you revert an applied batch later, even after a restart. Undo All lists conflicts and offers to restore under a new name.

### Is Zush or Wisfile better for invoices and receipts?

Zush, if you want a consistent pattern such as `date_vendor_amount` and automatic naming for new files. Its Bookkeeper Expenses Template is built for that, and Monitor can name scans as they land in a folder. Wisfile can rename PDF invoices once, but has no monitoring.
