## What Advanced Renamer does well

Advanced Renamer 4.28 (October 27, 2026) runs on Windows and Mac. Its strengths are real:

- **13 renaming methods you can stack.** Add, remove, replace, change case, renumber or build new names, combined in one batch and previewed before running.
- **Metadata-driven names.** It pulls EXIF date and time, image dimensions, camera model and GPS data into filenames, which suits photographers with consistent `DSC_*.NEF` archives.
- **Scripting.** Script methods can extract text from PDF and TXT files and use it in the name.
- **Move and copy rules.** Files can be relocated based on their information in the same batch.
- **Batch undo.** Regret a run, revert the whole thing.
- **Free for personal use.** Business use needs a license: $30 personal (VAT included) or $50 commercial (excluding VAT), one-time.

It holds 4.2 from 23 ratings on the Microsoft Store (US) and 3.5/5 from Softpedia's editors. If your photos have reliable EXIF and the desired name is `2026-06-12 14.32 Osaka.jpg`, Advanced Renamer may be all you need.

## Where metadata rules stop

Metadata answers when, where and with what a file was made, not what it shows or says. That gap covers most everyday mess:

- Screenshots: EXIF gives you the timestamp you already had, not "Stripe Dashboard Payout Error".
- Scanned PDFs: an image-only scan has no text for a script to extract, and Advanced Renamer has no OCR.
- Mixed client folders: no counter or GPS tag turns `Untitled.fig` and `meeting_notes_FINAL.docx` into names a teammate can search for.

Advanced Renamer's script methods can pull text out of a text-based PDF, but you still write the logic that picks the vendor or invoice number. For names that describe content, you need AI analysis. Our guide to [renaming files by content](/blog/rename-files-by-content-guide) shows what that looks like.

### When you also need folders organized

Zush also offers optional **AI folder sorting**. For a mixed batch of invoices, scans and photos, choose **Auto** grouping or write **Folder rules** such as “Group invoices by company and photos by subject.” The AI proposes folders from content while Naming Blocks still control each filename.

Choose a **Destination folder**, or keep the source folder as the root. Enable **Reuse existing folders** to prefer suitable folders already there. In a manual batch, review both the suggested names and folders before applying. To rename in place, leave sorting off.

Save the settings in a **Template** and assign it to **Monitor** for new files in the watched folder. Changing the Monitor Template does not reorganize files already processed. See the [folder sorting guide](/docs/folder-sorting).

## FAQ

### What is the best Advanced Renamer alternative?

[Zush](/) is our pick when files need names based on their content: it reads screenshots, scans, documents, photos, audio and video, and previews and records every batch on Mac and Windows. If your names come from metadata or a fixed rule, Bulk Rename Utility (Windows), A Better Finder Rename (Mac) or the free PowerRename and Finder tools are closer replacements. We make Zush, so weigh that.

### Is Advanced Renamer free?

For personal use, yes. Its download page says you can use it for free for personal use. Business use needs a license after trying it: $30 for the personal license (VAT included) or $50 for the commercial license (excluding VAT), both one-time. Zush, by comparison, gives 50 free AI renames and costs $48 one-time with cloud AI included.

### Is Advanced Renamer available for Mac?

Yes. Advanced Renamer runs on Windows and macOS, and version 4.28 was released for both on October 27, 2026. Many "Advanced Renamer for Mac" searches predate the macOS version. On a Mac you can also use Finder's built-in renamer, A Better Finder Rename for patterns and metadata, or Zush for names based on file content.

### Can Advanced Renamer name files based on their content?

Only partly. Script methods can extract text from PDF and TXT files, but it has no AI and no OCR, so it cannot read an image-only scan or describe what a photo or screenshot shows. You also write the logic yourself. Naming a file by what it shows or says is what the [AI batch rename workflow](/batch-rename-files) does, with review before applying and full undo.

### Does Zush upload my files when it renames them?

Only in cloud modes. With Zush Cloud AI or your own API key, file content is sent to that model to generate a name. With Ollama or LM Studio, analysis stays on your computer. Advanced Renamer, Bulk Rename Utility, PowerRename and Finder work entirely locally because they only apply rules and metadata, never AI analysis.

### Can Zush add counters and dates like Advanced Renamer?

Yes, as part of a Template. Naming Blocks add sequence numbers with a custom start and leading zeros, numbered per folder, plus dates, capture metadata, case and separators, next to the part of the name the AI writes. What Zush doesn't copy is Advanced Renamer's stack of scripted renaming methods. If the whole filename can be built from existing metadata or a fixed rule, Advanced Renamer remains the better fit.
