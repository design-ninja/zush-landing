## Zush

![Zush AI rename results on macOS showing content-aware filenames for mixed files](/images/showcase/macos/batch-rename-light.webp)

Zush works right away: download it, drop in files, and the first 50 AI renames run on managed Zush Cloud AI with no account, card, API key, or model download. The $48 one-time license keeps that managed cloud and adds unlimited renames, BYOK, Ollama, LM Studio, all future updates, and up to 3 devices across Mac and Windows. You never have to decide whether a messy 800-file folder is worth the credits.

Its main strength is naming control. Built-in Templates cover Screenshots, Designer Assets, Bookkeeper Expenses, Photographer Shoots, Video Production, Music Tracks, Movies & TV, Remove Duplicates, and more. They combine with 145+ Naming Blocks, Custom AI Blocks, sequence numbers, case, separators, date formats, language, Finder tags, and Spotlight metadata. You preview every name, can double-click one to edit it, and nothing changes until you click Rename. Rename History keeps the original and new names so a batch can be reverted later.

The 100+ formats include RAW and HEIC photos, Sketch/Figma/Illustrator/PSD files, iWork, Office and EPUB documents, audio, and video. Videos are named from speech, embedded subtitles, and metadata, including MKV. [Duplicate detection](/remove-duplicate-files) runs inside AI Rename, and copies use no credits.

You can [export Templates](/docs/template-import-export) with Naming Blocks, Custom AI Blocks, and folder rules, then import them on another computer.

### Content-based folders in Zush

Zush can **sort files into folders with AI** as well as rename them. Choose **Auto** or write **Folder rules**, for example "Group client documents by company and recordings by meeting topic." Folder grouping is independent of the filename Template, so the same batch can get consistent names and useful destinations. Zush also sorts into nested folders without renaming, with custom rules on size, dates, camera, duration, or page count.

Save the sorting settings in a **Template** and assign it to **Monitor** for new files. On Mac, monitoring also covers external drives and resumes after you reconnect them. Changing a Monitor Template does not reorganize files already processed. See the [folder sorting guide](/docs/folder-sorting) for the setup.

FilesDesk (version 1.0.35) sells managed AI as a credit subscription: 15 free credits after you sign in, then $8/month for 500 credits or $20/month for 2,000.

Yearly billing is $80 or $200 for the two tiers, and unused yearly credits expire when the year ends.

Its FAQ lists common images, PDFs and Office documents, MP4/MOV/AVI/MKV/WebM video, and MP3/WAV/FLAC/AAC/M4A audio. Processing History can undo completed renames.

The $40 Self-Managed license works with Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, or OpenRouter keys, or with local Ollama, LM Studio, or vLLM. It binds to one device, includes 1 year of updates, and has a 3-day refund window. Managed AI is not part of it.

**Choose FilesDesk if** you want attachments from Gmail, iCloud, or IMAP mail renamed as they arrive. Watch Inbox is new and Zush has no equivalent. vLLM support and Organize Flow's conditional moves and copies are other reasons to evaluate it.

### Organize Flow and practical limits

Organize Flow runs when a file arrives or after AI renaming. Conditions match extension, name, size, type, folder, and dates using All/Any logic. Actions can move or copy into date-based folders, add prefixes, suffixes, or timestamps, and write Finder tags, colors, and comments on Mac.

The main limit is the operating model. Managed AI needs a sign-in and spends a credit per AI name suggestion (re-running a file uses another), while the one-time path needs your own key or a local model. Its public docs don't list RAW, design, or iWork formats.

## How to choose

- **You rename a lot, or in bursts.** A 1,500-file photo import uses three months of FilesDesk Basic credits in an afternoon. Zush PRO has no meter.
- **You want the cheapest start without setup.** Zush's free tier needs no account; FilesDesk's needs a sign-in. Both need no card.
- **Your folders include specialist formats.** Both list video and audio, but only Zush publishes RAW camera, design, and iWork coverage.
- **You want email attachments handled.** FilesDesk's Watch Inbox does this; Zush doesn't.
- **You want precise conventions.** Zush has 145+ Naming Blocks, Custom AI Blocks, and per-folder Templates; FilesDesk documents naming templates without the same field-level control.
- **Safety net.** Zush's refund window is 14 days; FilesDesk Self-Managed has 3.

## FAQ

### Is FilesDesk cheaper than Zush?

The sticker prices buy different things. FilesDesk's $40 Self-Managed license binds to one device, includes 1 year of updates, and needs your own API key or local model. Zush's $48 one-time license includes managed Zush Cloud AI, BYOK, and local models, unlimited renames, all future updates, and up to 3 devices.

FilesDesk's cheapest managed tier is capped at 500 credits a month. Zush has no rename meter on either paid plan.

### Do I need an account to start?

For FilesDesk, yes: its managed AI and 15 free credits require a sign-in. Zush's 50 free AI renames run on managed Zush Cloud AI with no account, card, API key, or model download, so you can test it on a real folder within a minute of installing. Neither app asks for a card to start.

### Where are my files processed?

Both apps offer cloud and local routes. Zush Cloud AI and FilesDesk's managed AI send file content to the vendor's cloud for naming. BYOK in either app sends it to the provider you pick. For processing that stays on your computer, use Ollama or LM Studio in Zush, or Ollama, LM Studio, or vLLM on FilesDesk's Self-Managed license. Local models need suitable hardware.

### Which supports more file types?

Both document video and audio support. Zush publishes a concrete list of 100+ formats including 11 RAW camera formats, Sketch, Figma, PSD, iWork, and EPUB, and names videos from speech and subtitles, including MKV. FilesDesk lists common image, PDF, Office, video, and audio formats but does not publish RAW, design, or iWork coverage. Test your own specialist files before buying either.

### Can I undo renames in both?

Yes. Zush keeps a persistent Rename History with the original name, new name, folder, and timestamp, and can revert a whole batch. Undo All lists any conflicts and offers to restore under a new name. FilesDesk documents undo from Processing History. In both apps, preview names before applying so you rarely need the undo.

### Does Zush have an equivalent to FilesDesk's Watch Inbox?

No. FilesDesk's Watch Inbox renames attachments from Gmail, iCloud or IMAP mail as they arrive, and Zush doesn't connect to email. Zush monitors folders instead, including folders on external drives on Mac, and applies a Template to each new file. If email attachments are your main job, FilesDesk is the better fit. If you already save attachments into a folder, a monitored folder in Zush covers the renaming step.
