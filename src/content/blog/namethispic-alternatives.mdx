## What NameThisPic does

[NameThisPic](https://www.namethispic.com/) is a web app. You upload images, it analyzes each one, and it returns SEO-friendly filenames like `three-candles-book-cozy-reading.jpeg` plus alt text. You download the renamed files one by one or as a ZIP.

| NameThisPic plan | Price | Images per month | Per batch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free demo | $0 | 3 per day | n/a |
| Pro | $9/mo | 150 | Up to 50 |
| Plus | $29/mo | 1,000 | Up to 50 |
| Enterprise | $79/mo | 5,000 | Up to 100 |

It supports JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP, and SVG, and works on phones as well as desktops.

## When an alternative makes more sense

The browser workflow has limits you notice at scale:

- **Upload, then download.** Every image goes up to a server and comes back as a new file you have to put back in place.
- **Monthly caps.** 150 images on the $9 plan runs out quickly on a product catalog or a photo shoot.
- **Common web formats only.** HEIC from iPhones and RAW camera files are not on the list.
- **Batches of 50 to 100.** A folder of 2,000 images becomes 20 or more rounds.

## 1. Zush: bulk image renaming on your computer

[Zush](/) reads each image and renames it where it already sits. No upload, no ZIP. Drop a folder of 500 product shots or a whole photo shoot in, review the names, apply.

For web and SEO work, a few things help:

- **Kebab-case and lowercase formatting**, so names come out as `blue-ceramic-coffee-mug-top-view.jpg`.
- **Naming Blocks** to add a brand, product line, or SKU before the AI description, so every file follows the same pattern.
- **60+ output languages**, useful for localized storefronts.
- **All the formats a camera or phone produces**: JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, AVIF, and RAW like CR3, NEF, ARW, and DNG.

AI can run through Zush Cloud AI, your own API key, or locally with Ollama or LM Studio if client images must stay on your machine. Rename History lets you revert a batch later.

What Zush does not do is write alt text. If your CMS needs alt text, generate it separately or in your CMS; the descriptive filename still helps image search and keeps your media library readable. ## 2. Renamer.ai: web or desktop, document-first

[Renamer.ai](https://renamer.ai/) has both a web app and a desktop app. It renames photos by content and is especially strong on documents and scans. All paid plans are monthly and capped at 200, 1,000, or 5,000 files. See [Renamer.ai alternatives](/blog/renamer-ai-alternatives).

## 3. AI Renamer: cheap one-time desktop option

[AI Renamer](https://airenamer.app/) is a desktop app with a $19 one-time local mode, or cloud credits at $10 per 200. It handles common image formats with custom prompts. Folder monitoring and rename history are not documented. See [Zush vs AI Renamer](/blog/zush-vs-airenamer).

## 4. RenameClick: offline with a bundled model

[RenameClick](https://rename.click/) includes a local vision model, so it renames images offline after a one-time model download. It supports JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP, AVIF, HEIC, TIFF, and SVG. The free plan gives 30 applied renames a month; Pro is $48 plus VAT lifetime. See [Zush vs RenameClick](/blog/zush-vs-renameclick).

## What makes a good image filename for SEO

Whatever tool you use, the rules are the same:

1. Describe what is in the image in 3 to 7 words: `red-trail-running-shoe-side-view.jpg`, not `IMG_4382.jpg`.
2. Use lowercase and hyphens. Google treats hyphens as word separators.
3. Put the most specific words first. The product or subject beats the adjectives.
4. Skip keyword stuffing. `shoe-shoes-best-running-shoe-buy.jpg` looks like spam.
5. Keep the extension that matches the file type.

## FAQ

### What is the best NameThisPic alternative?

Zush, if you rename images in bulk. It is a desktop app for Mac and Windows that renames images in place, supports HEIC and RAW, and has no monthly cap on its paid plan. NameThisPic is still the better pick if you need alt text generated.

### Is NameThisPic free?

It has a free demo limited to 3 images a day. Paid plans start at $9 a month for 150 images.

### Do descriptive image filenames help SEO?

Yes. Google's image guidelines recommend descriptive filenames, and a name like `red-trail-running-shoe.jpg` gives search engines more context than `IMG_4382.jpg`. Alt text and surrounding page text matter too.

### Can I rename images for SEO without uploading them?

Yes. Desktop apps like Zush analyze images on your computer. With Ollama or LM Studio selected, the analysis also stays local.
