## What NameThisPic does

NameThisPic is a web app. You upload images, it analyzes each one, and it returns SEO-friendly filenames like `three-candles-book-cozy-reading.jpeg` plus alt text. You download the renamed files one by one or as a ZIP.

| NameThisPic plan | Price | Images per month | Per batch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free demo | $0 | 3 per day | n/a |
| Pro | $9/mo | 150 | Up to 50 |
| Plus | $29/mo | 1,000 | Up to 50 |
| Enterprise | $79/mo | 5,000 | Up to 100 |

It supports JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP and SVG, and works on phones as well as desktops.

## When an alternative makes more sense

The browser workflow has limits you notice at scale:

- **Upload, then download.** Every image goes up to a server and comes back as a new file you have to put back in place.
- **Monthly caps.** 150 images on the $9 plan runs out quickly on a product catalog or a photo shoot.
- **Common web formats only.** HEIC from iPhones and RAW camera files are not on the list.
- **Batches of 50 to 100.** A folder of 2,000 images becomes 20 or more rounds.

## 1. Zush: bulk image renaming on your computer

[Zush](/) reads each image and renames it where it is, as long as folder sorting is off and the original destination is kept. No browser upload or ZIP download is involved. Drop in a folder of 500 product shots, review the names, and click Rename. Nothing changes before that, and you can double-click any suggested name to edit it.

For web and SEO work, a few things help:

- **Kebab-case and lowercase formatting**, so names come out as `blue-ceramic-coffee-mug-top-view.jpg`.
- **Naming Blocks** to add a brand, product line or SKU before the AI description, so every file follows the same pattern.
- **60+ output languages**, useful for localized storefronts.
- **Camera and phone formats**: JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, AVIF, and RAW such as CR3, NEF, ARW and DNG.
- **Duplicates handled in the same pass.** AI Rename can spot exact copies from repeated exports and keep them, mark them, or move them to the Trash. Copies use no credits. See [remove duplicate files](/remove-duplicate-files).

It works right away: 50 free AI renames on managed Zush Cloud AI, with no account, card, API key or model download. The $48 one-time license includes that cloud AI with no cap, plus your own key or local models through Ollama or LM Studio, all future updates, and up to 3 devices across Mac and Windows. Rename History keeps every batch, so you can undo it later.

What Zush does not do is write alt text. If your CMS needs it, generate it separately; a descriptive filename still helps image search and keeps your media library readable. See [renaming photos for social media and the web](/blog/rename-photos-for-social-media).

For a product shoot, Zush can also sort images into folders by a rule such as "Group product photos by product category." Sorting moves files on disk; it does not update your CMS. See the [folder sorting guide](/docs/folder-sorting).

## 2. Renamer.ai: web or desktop, document-first

Renamer.ai has a web app and a desktop app for Mac and Windows. It renames photos by content, including HEIC, and is strongest on documents and scans. Camera RAW is not on its format list. Local mode is available on every plan, including the free Starter tier with 25 files a month. Paid plans are $9.95, $29.95 and $99.95 a month for 200, 1,000 and 5,000 files, with 15% off annual billing.

Choose it if you want a web option and also rename documents. See [Renamer.ai alternatives](/blog/renamer-ai-alternatives).

## 3. AI Renamer: cheap one-time desktop option

AI Renamer runs on Mac, Windows and Linux. It costs $19 once for Local mode (your own key or a local model through Ollama or LM Studio) or $10 per 200 cloud credits, and gives 10 free credits after a Google sign-in. Version 1.10.0 (November 15, 2026) added HEIC, AVIF, BMP and RAW photos, along with Name Templates. Version 1.9.0 added a Watch Folder beta that runs only while the app is open.

Its weak point: it shows a History list, but undo is not documented, so test on copies. See [Zush vs AI Renamer](/blog/zush-vs-airenamer).

## 4. RenameClick: offline with a bundled model

RenameClick includes a local vision model of about 4 GB, so it renames images offline after the download; the vendor recommends 16 GB of RAM. It supports JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP, AVIF, BMP, SVG, HEIC, HEIF and TIFF, but not camera RAW. The free plan gives 100 renames or moves a month, and Pro is $8 plus VAT a month or $36 plus VAT lifetime, running on its local model or your own key.

Its weak point: undo keeps 10 entries in memory and clears them on restart. See [Zush vs RenameClick](/blog/zush-vs-renameclick).

## What makes a good image filename for SEO

Whatever tool you use, the rules are the same:

1. Describe what is in the image in 3 to 7 words: `red-trail-running-shoe-side-view.jpg`, not `IMG_4382.jpg`.
2. Use lowercase and hyphens. Google treats hyphens as word separators.
3. Put the most specific words first. The product or subject beats the adjectives.
4. Skip keyword stuffing. `shoe-shoes-best-running-shoe-buy.jpg` looks like spam.
5. Keep the extension that matches the file type.

## FAQ

### What is the best NameThisPic alternative?

Zush, if you rename images in bulk. It is a desktop app for Mac and Windows that renames images in place, supports HEIC and RAW, and has no monthly cap on its paid plans. Its $48 one-time license includes managed cloud AI. NameThisPic is still the better pick if you need alt text generated along with the filename.

### Is NameThisPic free?

It has a free demo limited to 3 images a day. Paid plans are $9 a month for 150 images, $29 for 1,000, and $79 for 5,000, with batches of up to 50 or 100 images. For a free desktop start, Zush gives 50 AI renames and RenameClick gives 100 renames or moves a month.

### Which alternative is the best value?

It depends on what the price includes. AI Renamer's $19 and RenameClick's $36 plus VAT lifetime licenses run on your own key or a local model. Zush's $48 one-time license includes managed cloud AI with no cap, plus BYOK and local models, all future updates and 3 devices. Renamer.ai and NameThisPic are monthly subscriptions with image caps.

### Do descriptive image filenames help SEO?

Yes. Google's image guidelines recommend descriptive filenames, and a name like `red-trail-running-shoe.jpg` gives search engines more context than `IMG_4382.jpg`. Alt text and the surrounding page text matter too, so a good filename works alongside them rather than replacing them.

### Can I rename images for SEO without uploading them?

Yes. In Zush, choose Ollama or LM Studio and image analysis stays on your computer. Zush Cloud AI and BYOK send the content needed for naming to a cloud model, so a desktop app alone does not mean offline processing. RenameClick's bundled model, AI Renamer's local mode and Renamer.ai's local mode also keep images on your machine.
