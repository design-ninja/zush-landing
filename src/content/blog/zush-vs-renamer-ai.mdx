Quick answer: pick [Zush](/) if you want to pay once, rename without a monthly file cap, use your own API key without an expensive tier, or handle RAW photos, design files and video. Pick Renamer.ai if you mostly rename documents and scans and want a web app you can use without installing anything. Both have local AI, folder monitoring, templates and undo, so those are not deciding factors.

## Zush

![Zush AI rename results showing files with descriptive before and after names and green checkmarks](/images/showcase/macos/batch-rename-light.webp)

Zush is a desktop AI file renamer for Mac and Windows. It reads each file, proposes a name, and changes nothing until you click Rename. Ready-made Templates, 145+ Naming Blocks and Custom AI Blocks decide which fields appear and in what order. Rename History stores the original and new name, folder and time of every batch, and Undo All restores it later, listing any conflicts.

The free tier gives 50 AI renames on managed Zush Cloud AI with no account, card, API key or model download. PRO is $10 a month or $48 once, and both remove the limit across Zush Cloud AI, BYOK, LM Studio and Ollama. The one-time license includes all future updates and covers up to 3 devices across Mac and Windows.

Zush also sorts files into folders by plain-language rules, for example "Group invoices by supplier and videos by topic", or by Auto grouping. AI Rename can detect duplicates in the same pass: keep them, mark them in the name, or move them to the Trash, without spending credits. See [AI folder sorting](/docs/folder-sorting) and [duplicates](/docs/duplicates).

## Renamer.ai

![Renamer.ai desktop app screenshot (image: renamer.ai)](/images/blog/competitors/renamer-ai.webp)

Renamer.ai is a document-focused renamer with a desktop app for Mac and Windows and a web interface. Its homepage shows desktop v5.0.6; the latest changelog entry is v5.0.4 (October 21, 2026). It lists 30+ formats: PDFs, Office files, common photos including HEIC, EPS, SVG and AI vectors, and email. OCR is central to how it reads scans.

It offers three processing modes. Cloud runs on Renamer.ai's servers. A local model runs on your device and is available on every plan, including the free Starter tier. Your own API key sends data to your provider, but only on the Ultimate plan. Magic Folders watch folders on every plan, and a template builder covers invoices, contracts, receipts and property documents.

## Pricing: subscription vs one-time

Renamer.ai has no one-time option. At the listed monthly prices, a year costs about $119 on Pro, $359 on Power User, and $1,199 on Ultimate. The pricing page also has an annual toggle with 15% off, so check the checkout for your exact total.

The bigger difference is what the money buys. Zush's $48 includes managed cloud AI with no file cap, plus BYOK and local models. On Renamer.ai, using your own key requires the $99.95 Ultimate plan, and every plan is sized by monthly files.

## Formats

For JPG, PNG, HEIC, PDF, DOCX and XLSX, both publish support.

Zush adds 11 camera RAW variants (CR2, CR3, NEF, ARW, DNG, ORF, RAF, RW2, PEF, SRW, SR2), design files (Sketch, Figma, Illustrator, Photoshop), iWork documents, EPUB, audio, and video. Video names come from frames, speech, embedded subtitles and metadata, including MKV files. See [supported file types](/docs/supported-file-types).

Renamer.ai lists EPS vectors, which Zush's list does not include, and it does not list RAW, PSD or video. If EPS files are your main job, Renamer.ai covers them directly.

## Privacy: where processing happens

Both apps let you keep analysis on your computer. Renamer.ai's local model runs on-device on every plan. Zush uses Ollama or LM Studio for the same purpose on Mac and Windows.

In cloud modes, files leave the machine. Renamer.ai's FAQ says desktop files go to its servers for analysis and are then deleted, and web uploads are kept for up to 24 hours. Zush Cloud AI and BYOK send the content needed for naming to the chosen cloud model. Pick the mode per folder if some files are sensitive.

## Web access

Renamer.ai has a web interface, useful on a locked-down work computer or for an occasional batch. Zush is desktop-only on Mac and Windows.

## Which one should you pick?

**Choose Zush if:**

- You want to pay once instead of monthly, with no file cap
- You want to use your own API key without moving to a $99.95/month plan
- You rename RAW photos, design files, video, audio or iWork documents
- You want a persistent Rename History and batch undo
- You use up to 3 Mac or Windows computers

**Choose Renamer.ai if:**

- You need a web app in addition to the desktop app
- Your work is mostly scanned documents, and 25 to 5,000 files a month fits your volume
- You want a built-in local model without installing Ollama or LM Studio

If your shortlist is Renamer.ai and NameQuick rather than Zush, [Renamer.ai vs NameQuick](/blog/renamer-ai-vs-namequick) compares their allowances, local modes and document rules.

## Moving from Renamer.ai to Zush

Run Zush's 50 free renames on a sample folder with your usual file types, then choose Monthly or One-Time PRO. Files Renamer.ai already renamed stay as they are; they are ordinary files on disk. If you are going the other way for the web app, start on the free Starter tier to check it covers your volume.

## FAQ

### Is Zush cheaper than Renamer.ai?

Over time, yes. Zush is $48 once or $10 a month. Renamer.ai has no one-time option; at its listed monthly prices a year of Pro is about $119 and Ultimate about $1,199, with 15% off for annual billing. Zush's $48 also includes managed cloud AI with no file cap, plus BYOK and local models on up to 3 devices.

### Can I use my own API key with Renamer.ai?

Only on the Ultimate plan, which is $99.95 a month and includes 5,000 files. With your own key, data goes straight to your chosen provider. Zush supports your own Gemini, OpenRouter, OpenAI or Claude key on every plan, including the free tier, and the $48 one-time license removes the rename limit.

### Does Renamer.ai work offline or keep files local?

Yes, in local mode, which is available on every plan, including the free Starter tier. Renamer.ai says the local model runs entirely on your device. Its cloud mode sends desktop files to its servers for analysis and then deletes them. Zush keeps analysis local with Ollama or LM Studio on Mac and Windows.

### Does Renamer.ai support RAW photos or video?

Its format list includes HEIC, but not camera RAW formats such as CR2 or NEF, not Photoshop files, and not video. Zush lists 11 RAW variants, PSD, Sketch and Figma files, and video including MP4, MOV and MKV. If you shoot RAW or rename screen recordings, test them in Zush's free tier.

### Do both have folder monitoring and undo?

Yes. Renamer.ai's Magic Folders watch folders on every plan, and it supports undo after a preview. Zush's Monitor applies a Template to new files, with optional folder sorting, and Rename History keeps every batch on disk so you can undo it later with conflicts listed.

## Verdict

Both are solid AI renamers with local AI, monitoring and undo.

Choose <BlogHighlight>Zush at $48 one-time</BlogHighlight> if you want no file cap, cloud AI included in the price, BYOK on any plan, and RAW, design and video coverage. Choose Renamer.ai if a web app matters, your work is mostly documents, and a monthly file allowance fits.
