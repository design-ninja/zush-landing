[Zush](/) is a desktop app for Mac and Windows that names files from their content. It covers 100+ formats: 41 image formats including HEIC, AVIF and camera RAW, 4 design formats (Sketch, Figma .fig, Illustrator .ai, PSD), 40 document formats including PDF, Office, iWork, OpenDocument and EPUB, video including MP4, MOV and MKV, and 7 audio formats. Videos are named from speech, embedded subtitles and metadata; [how to rename video files with AI](/blog/rename-video-files-with-ai) explains what it reads.

How it works: pick a ready-made Template (Smart Rename, Screenshots, Movies & TV, Remove Duplicates and others) or build your own from 145+ Naming Blocks and Custom AI Blocks. Zush proposes a name for each file. You double-click any name to edit it, and nothing changes until you click Rename.

**Strengths:**

- Works on first launch with managed cloud AI, no account or key
- Four AI routes in one workflow: Zush Cloud AI, BYOK (Gemini, OpenRouter, OpenAI, Claude), Ollama, LM Studio, with local speech recognition for video through Ollama or LM Studio
- Preview before apply, persistent Rename History with batch undo, and Undo All that flags conflicts
- Duplicate detection: keep copies, mark them in the name, or move them to Trash/Recycle Bin, without spending credits
- Folder monitoring, including external drives on Mac that resume after reconnecting
- Sequence numbers with a custom start and leading zeros, numbered per folder
- Optional AI folder sorting with nested folders
- Light in cloud mode: about 200 MB, 4 GB RAM minimum
- Interface in 11+ languages; names in 60+ languages

**Limitations:**

- No Linux build and no browser-only version
- Zush Cloud AI and BYOK need internet; Ollama and LM Studio run on your computer
- Music identification without tags is Windows-only for now

**Best for:** Mac and Windows users with mixed folders who want AI renaming to work immediately and want to pay once.

#### Optional AI folder sorting

Zush can also sort files into folders. Choose **Auto** or write **Folder rules** such as "Group invoices by company and receipts by month." Rules can create nested folders and use file details such as dates and page counts (the Mac app also uses size, camera and duration). You can sort files without renaming them, choose a destination folder, and reuse existing folders. Save the setup in a Template and assign it to a monitored folder for new arrivals. ### 2. FilesDesk

FilesDesk runs on Mac and Windows with Watch Folders, a Watch Inbox that renames email attachments from Gmail, iCloud or IMAP, templates, and history with undo. It starts with 15 free credits after sign-in. Managed AI is subscription-only: $8/month for 500 credits or $20/month for 2,000 ($80 or $200 yearly), at 1 credit per AI name suggestion; re-running a file uses another credit. The $40 Self-Managed license uses your own key or a local model (Ollama, LM Studio, vLLM), binds to 1 device, includes one year of updates, and has a 3-day refund window.

**Choose FilesDesk if** you want email attachments renamed as they arrive. **Weakness:** its one-time license does not include managed AI. See [Zush vs FilesDesk](/blog/zush-vs-filesdesk).

### 3. RenameClick

RenameClick (now at rename.click) runs on Mac and Windows. Version 4.1.2 added video for MP4, MOV, M4V and 3GP using frames and local speech transcription; MKV, WebM and AVI are not supported, and neither are camera RAW or design files. The free tier allows 100 renames or moves per month with unlimited analysis. The Lifetime license is $36 + VAT for up to 3 devices on direct builds, and provider fees are not included.

**Choose RenameClick if** you want a bundled local model that needs no Ollama setup. **Weakness:** that model is about a 4 GB download with 16 GB RAM recommended, and Undo keeps only 10 entries in memory, cleared on restart. See [Zush vs RenameClick](/blog/zush-vs-renameclick).

### 4. AI Renamer

AI Renamer (airenamer.app) is a desktop app for Mac, Windows and now Linux. Version 1.9 added a Watch Folder (beta) that runs only while the app is open and skips subfolders; version 1.10 added video, audio, RAW, HEIC, Office and EPUB. Local Mode is $19 one-time with your own key or Ollama/LM Studio; cloud credits are $10 per 200, and 10 come free after a Google sign-in. No refund is stated and credit purchases are final. The free GPL-3.0 `ozgrozer/ai-renamer` CLI on GitHub is an unrelated project.

**Choose AI Renamer if** you need Linux or the lowest one-time price for local AI. **Weakness:** it documents no undo. See [Zush vs AI Renamer](/blog/zush-vs-airenamer).

### 5. Renamer.ai

Renamer.ai offers Windows and Mac apps plus a web interface, with Magic Folders, templates, preview and undo. Local mode is available on every tier, including the free Starter plan (25 files/month). Paid cloud plans are Pro $9.95/month (200 files), Power $29.95 (1,000) and Ultimate $99.95 (5,000), 15% less on annual billing. BYOK is only on Ultimate. It holds a 4.5/5 Softpedia editorial score.

**Choose Renamer.ai if** you want document templates and free local processing. **Weakness:** there is no one-time license, so managed AI is a monthly meter. See [Zush vs Renamer.ai](/blog/zush-vs-renamer-ai).

### 6. NameQuick

NameQuick (version 3.0.57, now also on the Mac App Store) runs on Mac and Windows with Watch Folders, Rules, Quick Organize and undo. It gives 30 free Managed renames without email or card; the 14-day Managed trial (70 renames) needs a card. Managed plans start at $12/month, with rename packs at $19 for 500 or $39 for 1,500, valid 12 months. Self-Managed is $69 one-time for one computer, with lifetime updates and a 14-day refund; AI costs are separate.

**Choose NameQuick if** you want a rules engine on top of AI naming. **Weakness:** the one-time license covers one computer and no managed AI. See [Zush vs NameQuick](/blog/zush-vs-namequick).

### 7. RenamerX

RenamerX is a local-first renamer for Mac and Windows. Its built-in model (Qwen3.5 4B) is about a 3.4 GB download and runs on Apple Silicon or on Windows x64 PCs with a compatible GPU. It also connects to Ollama, LM Studio, any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, or OpenAI and Gemini with your own key. Its site lists documents, images including camera RAW, and video including MKV, plus Watch Folders and undo. Free gives 50 credits a month; Pro is $36 lifetime with unlimited credits. Device count, refund terms and update policy are not stated on its homepage.

**Choose RenamerX if** you want RAW and MKV support with a bundled local model. **Weakness:** there is no managed cloud, and audio files are not listed.

### 8. Wisfile

Wisfile is free and runs 100% locally on Mac and Windows, with a focus on PDF and Word files. It has no undo.

**Choose Wisfile if** you rename documents occasionally and want no cost and no cloud. **Weakness:** no undo means a bad batch has to be fixed by hand.

### 9. Renamed.to

Renamed.to is a cloud service with an API and CLI. It starts with 50 free renames, then sells credits at $9 for 1,000 or $49 for 10,000, valid for 12 months.

**Choose Renamed.to if** your documents live in cloud storage or you need an API. **Weakness:** it is cloud-only, so there is no local processing route.

### 10. File Renamer AI

File Renamer AI is a browser tool: upload files, review names, download a ZIP. Credits cost $10 for 1,000 or $100 for 10,000 and never expire. Its homepage now mentions PDFs, Office, video and audio, while its pricing page still describes images.

**Choose File Renamer AI if** you cannot install software. **Weakness:** no desktop app, no folder monitoring, and no undo after the ZIP download.

## FAQ

### What is an AI file renamer?

An AI file renamer is an app that reads the content of each file and suggests a descriptive name for it. A pattern tool can add a date or replace text across a batch, but it cannot tell an invoice from a contract when both are called `scan_004.pdf`. An AI renamer reads the text, image, audio or video and gives each file its own name, which you should be able to review before anything changes.

### What is the best AI file renamer in 2026?

Zush is our pick for Mac and Windows. It works on first launch with 50 free AI renames and no account, card or API key. Its $48 one-time license includes managed cloud AI, BYOK, Ollama and LM Studio on up to 3 devices, and every batch is previewed and kept in Rename History with batch undo. AI Renamer is the Linux option, and Wisfile is free and local on Mac and Windows; Renamed.to fits cloud-storage and API workflows.

### Is a $48 one-time license worth more than a $19 or $36 one?

It depends on what you want the AI to run on. The $19 AI Renamer, $36 RenameClick and $36 RenamerX licenses cover the app, but the AI is your own API key or a local model your computer has to run. Zush's $48 includes managed Zush Cloud AI with unlimited renames, plus BYOK and local models, all future updates, 3 devices across Mac and Windows, and a 14-day refund.

### Where are my files processed?

That depends on the mode. With Zush Cloud AI or BYOK, file content is sent to the AI service to generate a name. With Ollama or LM Studio, analysis runs on your own computer. RenameClick and RenamerX default to a bundled local model; Renamer.ai offers local mode on every tier; Wisfile is local only; Renamed.to and File Renamer AI are cloud services. Check each vendor's privacy page for retention terms.

### Which AI file renamers work on Linux?

Of the tools compared here, AI Renamer now publishes Linux builds. Zush, FilesDesk, RenameClick, NameQuick, RenamerX and Wisfile have Mac and Windows apps but no Linux version, as checked November 17, 2026; Wisfile lists Linux as coming soon. Browser and API tools such as Renamed.to and File Renamer AI work from any operating system, but your files go to their cloud service.

### Can I undo an AI rename?

In Zush, yes: Rename History is persistent, so you can undo a whole batch days later, and Undo All lists any conflicts before restoring. FilesDesk and NameQuick also keep history with undo. RenameClick keeps 10 undo entries in memory that clear on restart. AI Renamer documents no undo and Wisfile has none, so test those on copies first.
