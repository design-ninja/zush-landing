## Why people switch from Renamer.ai

[Renamer.ai](https://renamer.ai/) runs as a desktop app (Mac and Windows) and on the web. It focuses on documents: PDFs, Office files, images, and vector formats, with OCR at the core and Magic Folders for automation.

Compare the published cloud allowance with the size and frequency of your batches:

| Renamer.ai plan | Price | Files per month |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 25 |
| Paid tiers | $9.95 to $99.95 per month | 200, 1,000, or 5,000 |

The published cloud tiers are metered. Renamer.ai now offers downloadable local models and OpenAI/Gemini BYOK; its pricing page does not clearly separate the license allowances for those modes. Confirm those terms before assuming that every local or BYOK operation consumes a cloud credit. At an unchanged $9.95/month, five uninterrupted years of cloud Pro total $597 before taxes or discounts.

**Renamer.ai 5.0.4**, released October 21, adds Activity filters, bulk retry, and Magic Folder recovery after external-drive disconnection. [Source: official changelog](https://renamer.ai/changelog). These improvements are reasons to test an existing setup before switching. Zush’s case is its combined format coverage, naming controls, recovery, and included cloud allowance.

## 1. Zush: mixed files with included cloud AI

[Zush](/) is a desktop app for Mac and Windows that reads each file and proposes a descriptive name. You review the whole batch, then apply it. Rename History retains original names and paths. Undo All shows restored files and problems, including an option to restore under a new name if the old location is occupied.

What makes it the closest replacement:

- **No file cap.** Paid PRO is unmetered, at $10 a month or $48 once. The free start includes 50 renames.
- **Four ways to run AI.** Zush Cloud AI, your own key for Gemini, OpenRouter, OpenAI, or Claude, or local models through Ollama or LM Studio.
- **Wider file coverage.** Beyond PDFs and Office files: RAW photos, HEIC, design files (PSD, Figma, Sketch, AI), 16 video formats, and audio.
- **Naming you control.** Templates and 145+ Naming Blocks combine fields such as date, vendor, amount, and client. Sequence Number adds a custom start and leading zeros, continuing separately in each folder.
- **Folder monitoring.** New files in Downloads or a scanner folder get named as they arrive, like Magic Folders.

Where Renamer.ai is still stronger: it has a web app, so you can rename without installing anything. Zush is desktop only. Full breakdown: [Zush vs Renamer.ai](/blog/zush-vs-renamer-ai).

### Organize a mixed batch by content

Zush also includes **AI folder sorting**. Use **Auto** or write **Folder rules**, for example “Group invoices by company, receipts by month, and videos by topic.” Filename Templates and Naming Blocks control what each file is called; the folder rule controls where it belongs. This extends the desktop workflow beyond naming new arrivals.

Choose a **Destination folder**, or keep the source folder as the root. Enable **Reuse existing folders** to prefer suitable folders already there; Zush can create a new one when nothing fits. In a manual batch, review both the suggested names and folders before applying. To rename in place, leave sorting off and keep the original destination.

Save the sorting settings in a **Template** and assign it to **Monitor** for new files directly inside the watched folder. Changing the Monitor Template does not automatically reorganize files already processed. Test the Template in a manual batch first. See the [folder sorting guide](/docs/folder-sorting) for the setup and monitoring scope.

Zush also supports nested folder rules, such as grouping by year and then month. You can sort already renamed files without changing their names again or spending another rename credit. Review the destinations before applying. This separate action handles an existing batch; changing a Monitor Template does not retroactively reorganize it. [AI folder sorting](/docs/folder-sorting) works on Mac and Windows.

## 2. RenameClick: best for a bundled local model

[RenameClick](https://rename.click/) downloads a local vision model on first run, for offline renaming after the model is installed and configured. It covers images, documents, and audio with local transcription, and its Auto Flow can rename and move files. The free tier currently allows 100 successful renames or moves a month.

At the October 25, 2026 check, Pro is $8 plus VAT per month or $36 plus VAT lifetime; verify the live checkout. Video, RAW, and design files are not in its published format list. See [Zush vs RenameClick](/blog/zush-vs-renameclick).

## 3. FilesDesk: best if you already have an API key

[FilesDesk](https://filesdesk.app/) uses credits: 1 credit per filename suggestion. Managed plans are $8 a month for 500 credits or $20 for 2,000. The $40 lifetime plan is unmetered but requires your own API key or a local model (Ollama, LM Studio, or vLLM).

It supports images, PDFs, Office files, video, and audio, with Watch Folders, undo, and [Organize Flow](https://filesdesk.app/docs/core-features/organize-flow) for conditional moves, copies, tags, and comments. Local options include Ollama, LM Studio, and vLLM. Important limits: subscriptions cannot use BYOK/local AI, credits reset monthly without rollover, and the self-managed license covers one device with 12 months of updates. See [Zush vs FilesDesk](/blog/zush-vs-filesdesk).

## 4. NameQuick: conditional document workflows

[NameQuick](https://www.namequick.app/) combines AI naming with Rules that can move, tag, or archive files after renaming. Managed plans start at $12 a month and are metered (500 to 10,000 renames). The $69 Self-Managed license removes metering if you bring a key or run Ollama, LM Studio, or MLX.

NameQuick ships on Mac and Windows; MLX, Finder tags, and comments are Mac-only. Managed starts with 30 free renames without email, card, or time limit; an optional additional 70 require a card and last 14 days. Self-Managed has its own 30-rename trial with your key or local model. Managed suggestions count when generated, even when not applied, and monthly unused allowance expires. See the current [product and pricing page](https://www.namequick.app/) and [Zush vs NameQuick](/blog/zush-vs-namequick).

## 5. Renamed.to: best for cloud storage workflows

[Renamed.to](https://renamed.to/) works inside Google Drive, Dropbox, and OneDrive, plus an API, CLI, and desktop file watcher. Processing is cloud-based; a watcher on your computer is not local AI. Check the current [integration catalog](https://www.renamed.to/) rather than assuming that every announced connector is available.

It charges per document: $9 for 1,000 or $49 for 10,000, with 50 free. It focuses on documents (PDF, Office, scans). See [Zush vs Renamed.to](/blog/zush-vs-renamed-to).

## FAQ

### Can Zush sort files into folders as well as rename them?

Yes. Its optional AI folder sorting works on Mac and Windows. Choose Auto or write Folder rules, select the destination, and review both names and groups in a manual batch. A sorting-enabled Template can also process new files directly inside a monitored folder; changing its Template does not automatically reorganize files already processed. Leave sorting off and keep the original destination for naming only.

### What is the best alternative to Renamer.ai?

Zush is our pick for mixed desktop archives. It renames files by content on Mac and Windows, has no monthly file cap on its paid plan ($48 one-time or $10 a month), supports local AI, and covers RAW photos, design files, and video.

### Is there a free Renamer.ai alternative?

Zush includes 50 free AI renames, and RenameClick currently gives 100 successful renames or moves a month for free. For unlimited free use, an open-source command-line tool like `ozgrozer/ai-renamer` with Ollama works if you are comfortable in a terminal.

### Does Renamer.ai work offline?

Yes: Renamer.ai v5 documents on-device processing through downloadable local models. Zush connects to Ollama or LM Studio; RenameClick offers a bundled model. Test the selected local model on representative files and confirm the license terms before choosing.

### Is Renamer.ai a one-time purchase?

Its public pricing lists recurring cloud subscriptions, not a one-time purchase. Local/BYOK license terms are not clearly separated there. Zush, RenameClick, FilesDesk, and NameQuick publish one-time options with different AI and update entitlements.
