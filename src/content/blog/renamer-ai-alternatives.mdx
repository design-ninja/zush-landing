Quick answer: the best Renamer.ai alternative for most people is [Zush](/). It covers the same core job, renaming files by what is inside them, without a monthly file cap. Its one-time plan is $48, it runs AI locally if you want, and it handles files Renamer.ai does not list, like RAW photos, Photoshop, Figma, and Sketch files, and video.

Renamer.ai is a solid document renamer. People usually start looking elsewhere for one of three reasons: the monthly file limits, the lack of a local or bring-your-own-key option, or file types it does not cover.

## Why people switch from Renamer.ai

[Renamer.ai](https://renamer.ai/) runs as a desktop app (Mac and Windows) and on the web. It focuses on documents: PDFs, Office files, images, and vector formats, with OCR at the core and Magic Folders for automation.

Its pricing is where most people hit a wall:

| Renamer.ai plan | Price | Files per month |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 25 |
| Paid tiers | $9.95 to $99.95 per month | 200, 1,000, or 5,000 |

Every paid tier is metered, and there is no published BYOK or local-only mode. If you rename a few hundred files a month, that adds up. At $9.95 a month, five years cost about $597.

## 1. Zush: best overall alternative

[Zush](/) is a desktop app for Mac and Windows that reads each file and proposes a descriptive name. You review the whole batch, then apply it. Rename History keeps every original name, so a batch can be reverted later.

What makes it the closest replacement:

- **No file cap.** Paid PRO is unmetered, at $10 a month or $48 once. The free start includes 50 renames.
- **Four ways to run AI.** Zush Cloud AI, your own key for Gemini, OpenRouter, OpenAI, or Claude, or local models through Ollama or LM Studio.
- **Wider file coverage.** Beyond PDFs and Office files: RAW photos, HEIC, design files (PSD, Figma, Sketch, AI), 16 video formats, and audio.
- **Naming you control.** Templates and 145+ Naming Blocks (date, vendor, amount, client, document type) keep names consistent.
- **Folder monitoring.** New files in Downloads or a scanner folder get named as they arrive, like Magic Folders.

Where Renamer.ai is still stronger: it has a web app, so you can rename without installing anything. Zush is desktop only. Full breakdown: [Zush vs Renamer.ai](/blog/zush-vs-renamer-ai).

## 2. RenameClick: best for a bundled local model

[RenameClick](https://rename.click/) downloads a local vision model on first run, so it renames offline without setup. It covers images, documents, and audio with local transcription, and its Auto Flow can rename and move files. A free tier allows 30 applied renames a month.

Pro is $8 plus VAT per month or $48 plus VAT lifetime. Video, RAW, and design files are not in its published format list. See [Zush vs RenameClick](/blog/zush-vs-renameclick).

## 3. FilesDesk: best if you already have an API key

[FilesDesk](https://filesdesk.app/) uses credits: 1 credit per filename suggestion. Managed plans are $8 a month for 500 credits or $20 for 2,000. The $40 lifetime plan is unmetered but requires your own API key or a local model (Ollama or vLLM).

It supports images, PDFs, Office files, video, and audio, with Watch Folders and an undo history. See [Zush vs FilesDesk](/blog/zush-vs-filesdesk).

## 4. NameQuick: best for document rules on Mac

[NameQuick](https://www.namequick.app/) combines AI naming with Rules that can move, tag, or archive files after renaming. Managed plans start at $12 a month and are metered (500 to 10,000 renames). The $69 Self-Managed license removes metering if you bring a key or run Ollama, LM Studio, or MLX.

It is strongest for recurring PDFs, receipts, and contracts. See [Zush vs NameQuick](/blog/zush-vs-namequick).

## 5. Renamed.to: best for cloud storage workflows

[Renamed.to](https://renamed.to/) works inside Google Drive, Dropbox, and OneDrive, plus an API and Zapier or Make integrations. There is no desktop app and no local processing.

It charges per document: $9 for 1,000 or $49 for 10,000, with 50 free. It focuses on documents (PDF, Office, scans). See [Zush vs Renamed.to](/blog/zush-vs-renamed-to).

## Which alternative fits you?

- Mixed files, no monthly cap, optional local AI: **Zush**.
- Offline AI with nothing to configure: **RenameClick**.
- You already pay for an OpenAI or Claude key: **FilesDesk** lifetime, or Zush with BYOK.
- Document rules that also move files on a Mac: **NameQuick**.
- Everything lives in Google Drive or Dropbox: **Renamed.to**.

## FAQ

### What is the best alternative to Renamer.ai?

Zush, for most people. It renames files by content on Mac and Windows, has no monthly file cap on its paid plan ($48 one-time or $10 a month), supports local AI, and covers RAW photos, design files, and video.

### Is there a free Renamer.ai alternative?

Zush includes 50 free AI renames, and RenameClick gives 30 applied renames a month for free. For unlimited free use, an open-source command-line tool like `ozgrozer/ai-renamer` with Ollama works if you are comfortable in a terminal.

### Does Renamer.ai work offline?

Renamer.ai does not publish a local-only mode. If files must stay on your computer, use Zush with Ollama or LM Studio, or RenameClick's built-in model.

### Is Renamer.ai a one-time purchase?

No. All Renamer.ai paid plans are monthly subscriptions with a file limit. Zush, RenameClick, FilesDesk, and NameQuick all offer a one-time option.
