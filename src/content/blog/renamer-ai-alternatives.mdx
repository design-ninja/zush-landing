## Why people switch from Renamer.ai

Renamer.ai runs as a desktop app on Mac and Windows and on the web. It focuses on documents and scans, with OCR at the core, built-in templates, Magic Folders on every plan, and undo. Its local model is available on every plan, including the free tier, and it has a Softpedia editor score of 4.5/5, the same as Zush.

The friction is in how it is sold:

| Renamer.ai plan | Listed price | Files per month |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | Free | 25 |
| Pro | $9.95/mo | 200 |
| Power User | $29.95/mo | 1,000 |
| Ultimate | $99.95/mo | 5,000 |

- **No one-time option.** At the listed monthly prices, a year of Pro is about $119 and five years about $597. Annual billing is advertised at 15% off.
- **Your own API key only on Ultimate**, at $99.95 a month.
- **Formats.** HEIC, Office, PDF and vectors are listed; camera RAW, PSD and video are not.

Ranked on: one-time versus monthly-capped pricing, whether managed AI and your own API key are included, file-format breadth, and preview with persistent undo.

## 1. Zush: best overall alternative

[Zush](/) is a desktop app for Mac and Windows that reads each file and proposes a descriptive name. Nothing changes until you click Rename, and Rename History keeps every batch on disk so you can undo it later.

What makes it the closest replacement:

- **No file cap, and the cloud is included.** PRO is $10 a month or $48 once. The one-time license covers managed Zush Cloud AI, BYOK and local models, all future updates, up to 3 devices across Mac and Windows, and a 14-day refund.
- **Works right away.** 50 free AI renames on Zush Cloud AI, with no account, card, API key or model download.
- **Your own key on any plan.** Gemini, OpenRouter, OpenAI or Claude, or local models through Ollama or LM Studio.
- **Wider file coverage.** Beyond PDFs and Office files: RAW photos, HEIC, design files (PSD, Figma, Sketch, AI), iWork, EPUB, audio, and video including MP4, MOV and MKV.
- **Naming you control.** Templates and 145+ Naming Blocks (date, vendor, amount, client, document type) keep names consistent, and Monitor applies them to new files, like Magic Folders.
- **Duplicates and folders in the same pass.** AI Rename can flag or trash duplicate copies without spending credits, and sort files into folders by rules you write. See [AI folder sorting](/docs/folder-sorting).

Where Renamer.ai is still stronger: it has a web app, so you can rename without installing anything. Zush is desktop-only. Full breakdown: [Zush vs Renamer.ai](/blog/zush-vs-renamer-ai).

## 2. RenameClick: best for a bundled local model

RenameClick, now at rename.click, ships a built-in local model of about 4 GB, so after the download it renames offline without installing Ollama. The vendor recommends 16 GB of RAM. It covers images, documents, audio, and since v4.1.2 video in MP4, MOV, M4V and 3GP. MKV, WebM, AVI, RAW and design files are not supported. Version 4.0.1 added an MCP server for AI agents.

The free plan gives 100 renames or moves a month. Pro is $8 plus VAT a month or $36 plus VAT lifetime; the lifetime license runs on its local model or your own key, with provider fees extra. Its weak point is recovery: standard undo keeps 10 entries in memory and clears them on restart. See [Zush vs RenameClick](/blog/zush-vs-renameclick).

## 3. FilesDesk: best if you already have an API key

FilesDesk charges 1 credit per AI name suggestion, and re-running a file uses another credit: $8 a month for 500 or $20 for 2,000, or $80 and $200 a year. The free tier has 15 credits and needs a sign-in. The $40 Self-Managed license is unmetered with your own Claude, OpenAI, Gemini or OpenRouter key, or with Ollama, LM Studio or vLLM.

It handles images, PDFs, Office files, video and audio, with Watch Folders, Watch Inbox for email attachments, and Processing History with undo. Limits to know: managed AI is subscription-only, the self-managed license binds to one device with 1 year of updates, and refunds are within 3 days. See [Zush vs FilesDesk](/blog/zush-vs-filesdesk).

## 4. NameQuick: conditional document workflows

NameQuick combines AI naming with Rules that move or tag files after renaming. It runs on Mac and Windows, and is also on the Mac App Store and Setapp.

Managed plans start at $12 a month for 500 renames and go up to 10,000. Rename packs cost $19 for 500 or $39 for 1,500, valid 12 months. You get 30 free Managed renames with no email or card; the 14-day Managed trial with 70 renames needs a card. The $69 Self-Managed license covers one computer with lifetime updates, using your own key (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, OpenRouter, Mistral) or Ollama, LM Studio or MLX. MLX and Finder tags are Mac-only.

Its weak point: undo can fail if a file has since moved. See [Zush vs NameQuick](/blog/zush-vs-namequick).

## 5. Renamed.to: best for cloud storage workflows

Renamed.to works inside Google Drive, Dropbox and OneDrive, with an API, a CLI, and Zapier or Make integrations. There is no desktop app and no local processing.

It charges per document: 50 free, then $9 for 1,000 or $49 for 10,000 credits, valid 12 months. It focuses on PDFs, Office files and scans. See [Zush vs Renamed.to](/blog/zush-vs-renamed-to).

## Which alternative fits you?

- Mixed files, no monthly cap, cloud AI included in a one-time price: **Zush**.
- Offline AI with no Ollama setup, on a machine with 16 GB of RAM: **RenameClick**.
- You already pay for an API key and use one computer: **FilesDesk** Self-Managed, or Zush with BYOK.
- Documents filed by rules after naming: **NameQuick**.
- Everything lives in Google Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive: **Renamed.to**.

## FAQ

### What is the best alternative to Renamer.ai?

Zush, for most people. It renames files by content on Mac and Windows, has no monthly file cap on its paid plans, and its $48 one-time license includes managed cloud AI plus your own key and local models. It also covers RAW photos, design files and video. Renamer.ai remains the better fit if you need a web app.

### Is Renamer.ai a one-time purchase?

No. Renamer.ai sells monthly plans sized by files: free Starter (25), Pro $9.95 (200), Power User $29.95 (1,000) and Ultimate $99.95 (5,000), with 15% off for annual billing. Zush ($48), RenameClick ($36 plus VAT), FilesDesk ($40) and NameQuick ($69) all sell one-time licenses, but only Zush's includes managed cloud AI.

### Does Renamer.ai work offline?

Yes. Its local model runs on your device and is available on every plan, including the free Starter tier. Cloud mode sends files to Renamer.ai's servers. Among the alternatives, Zush works offline with Ollama or LM Studio, RenameClick has a bundled model, and FilesDesk and NameQuick run local models on their self-managed licenses. Renamed.to is cloud-only.

### Can I use my own API key with Renamer.ai?

Only on the Ultimate plan, at $99.95 a month. Zush accepts your own Gemini, OpenRouter, OpenAI or Claude key on every plan, including the free tier. FilesDesk and NameQuick support your own key on their self-managed licenses, and RenameClick supports OpenAI-compatible APIs in its direct builds.

### Is there a free Renamer.ai alternative?

Zush gives 50 free AI renames on managed cloud with no account. RenameClick gives 100 renames or moves a month after a ~4 GB model download, and NameQuick gives 30 free managed renames. For unlimited free use, the open-source `ozgrozer/ai-renamer` command-line tool works with Ollama if you are comfortable in a terminal.
