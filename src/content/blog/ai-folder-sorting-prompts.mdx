Quick answer: a folder sorting prompt tells AI how to group files and how to name the folders, in one or two plain sentences. For example: "Group invoices by company and receipts by month. Name month folders YYYY-MM." The prompts below work in [Zush](/ai-file-sorter) **Folder rules** on Mac and Windows, where the field accepts up to 2,000 characters. Zush reads each file, renames it with your Template, and moves it into the folder your prompt describes, after you review the plan.

## The formula for a good folder rule

A reliable prompt answers three questions:

1. **Which detail groups the files?** Company, client, month, year, subject, document type, project.
2. **How should the folders be named?** "Use the company name," "Name folders YYYY-MM," "Use short topic names."
3. **What is the exception?** "Put personal documents in Personal," "Keep screenshots separate."

Write it the way you would brief a new assistant. No conditions, wildcards, or scripts. Keep the detail visible in the file: a company name on an invoice is easy; "urgent vs not urgent" is guesswork.

| Weak prompt | Better prompt |
|---|---|
| Organize everything. | Group files by document type: Invoices, Receipts, Contracts, Manuals, Images. |
| Sort by importance. | Put tax documents in Taxes and everything else by year. |
| Folders for each file. | Group invoices by supplier. Use a few broad folders, not one per file. |

## Finance and paperwork

1. Group invoices by company and receipts by month. Name month folders YYYY-MM.
2. Group invoices by supplier and use the supplier's company name for folders.
3. Sort receipts by expense category: Meals, Travel, Software, Office Supplies, Hardware, Other.
4. Group bank and card statements by bank name.
5. Group tax documents by tax year, such as 2025 and 2026. Put forms like W-2, 1099, and 1098 in the year they cover, not the year they were received.
6. Separate payroll documents from invoices and receipts. Name folders Payroll, Invoices, Receipts.
7. Group utility bills by provider and service, such as Electricity, Water, Internet, Phone.

Pair these with a naming Template such as `Date – Vendor – Amount` so each file is findable inside its folder. See [how to organize invoices and receipts](/blog/how-to-organize-invoices-and-receipts).

## Clients and work projects

8. Group documents by client company. Use the client's company name for folders.
9. Group contracts, proposals, and statements of work by client, and put anything without a client name in Unassigned.
10. Sort files by project name mentioned in the document or visible in the screenshot.
11. Group meeting notes and recordings by the team or client discussed.
12. Group design files and exports by brand or product shown in them.

## Photos and images

13. Group photos by subject, such as People, Pets, Food, Landscapes, and Documents.
14. Group photos by event or trip, using short names like "Lisbon Trip" or "Birthday Party".
15. Separate screenshots, scanned documents, and real photos into Screenshots, Scans, and Photos.
16. Group product photos by product type, such as Shoes, Bags, and Accessories.
17. Group property photos by street address visible in the listing documents or signs.

## Screenshots and Downloads

18. Group screenshots by the app or website shown, such as Figma, Slack, Stripe, and Browser.
19. Group screenshots by topic: Bugs, Designs, Receipts, Conversations, Charts.
20. Group downloads by document type: Invoices, Receipts, Contracts, Manuals, Installers, Images, Other.
21. Put installers and disk images in Installers, and sort everything else by document type.

For a hands-off Downloads folder, save the rule in a Template and assign it to Monitor. See [automate your Downloads folder](/automate-downloads-folder).

## Video and audio

22. Group videos by topic and use short, descriptive folder names.
23. Group screen recordings by the app or product shown on screen.
24. Separate B-roll, interviews, and screen recordings into three folders.
25. Group voice memos and recordings by topic, such as Meetings, Ideas, Lectures, and Calls.
26. Group music and audio files by mood or genre, such as Ambient, Upbeat, Voiceover, and Sound Effects.

Zush looks at sampled video frames and audio content, so these rules work even when every file is called `IMG_5501.MOV` or `New Recording 14.m4a`.

## Professions

27. **Accountants:** Group client documents by client name. Use the company name for businesses and the family name for individuals.
28. **Legal:** Group documents by matter or case name found in the document. Put anything without a matter in Needs Review.
29. **Real estate:** Group transaction documents by property address. Use the street address for folder names.
30. **Students:** Group notes, slides, and assignments by course name or course code.

For professional archives, keep the prompt to details printed in the document. Do not ask AI to infer sensitive traits; Zush's folder planner is instructed to avoid them anyway.

## How Zush applies your prompt

- **Auto first.** Leave Folder rules empty to let Zush group files by content into a few broad folders. Write a prompt when you know how you want them split.
- **Destination.** Folders are created inside the destination you choose, or next to the files if you keep the original location. Each batch creates one level of folders there.
- **Existing folders.** With **Reuse existing folders** on, Zush prefers matching folders that already exist and creates new ones only when nothing fits.
- **Review.** You see every proposed folder and filename before anything moves. Rename a folder or move a file if needed. Undo moves a file back to its original folder and name.
- **Automation.** In Monitor, the rule applies to each new file. Zush remembers which files belong in each folder it created, so future files keep landing consistently.
- **Language.** New folder names follow your naming language setting.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Too many small folders | Add "Use a few broad folders" or list the folder names you want. |
| Folder names are inconsistent | Specify the format: "Use the company name only, without Inc or LLC." |
| Files go to the wrong folder | Check that the detail is visible in the file. Scans with little text or blurry photos need review. |
| Some files stay ungrouped | They did not clearly fit any folder. Add a catch-all: "Put everything else in Other." |
| Existing folders are ignored | Turn on Reuse existing folders, and make sure the folders sit directly inside the destination. |

## FAQ

### What is a folder sorting prompt?

A folder sorting prompt is a plain-language instruction that tells an AI file sorter how to group files into folders and how to name them, such as "Group invoices by company and receipts by month." In Zush, you paste it into Folder rules, and Zush applies it to every file in the batch or in a monitored folder.

### How long can a Zush folder rule be?

Up to 2,000 characters. Most good rules are one to three sentences. Leave the field empty to use Auto grouping.

### Can one prompt create folders and rename files?

In Zush, the prompt controls the folders and your Template controls the filenames, and both are applied in the same reviewed batch. Use [custom rename prompts](/docs/custom-prompts) to control the wording of the filenames themselves.

### Can I use these prompts in other AI file sorters?

Most of them work in any prompt-based sorter, such as Sortio or Sparkle. Category-based tools like RenameClick need a fixed list of folder names instead of a sentence.

### Do folder sorting prompts work offline?

In Zush, yes. With LM Studio or Ollama selected, both file analysis and folder planning run on your computer, so the prompt and the files stay local.
