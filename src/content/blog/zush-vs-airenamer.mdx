Quick answer: **[Zush](/) is the better pick for most people on Mac and Windows**, because every batch stays reversible in Rename History, the $48 one-time price includes managed cloud AI, and the first rename needs no sign-in. Pick AI Renamer Desktop for the cheapest one-time license ($19, your own key or a local model) or for Linux.

**How we checked:** Written by the maker of [Zush](/). Competitor prices, features and ratings were checked on vendor sites and official stores on November 17, 2026 ([sources below](#sources-and-how-we-checked)). This compares documented features, not a lab test.

Both apps now watch folders and handle video, RAW and Office files, so the decision is about recovery, what the license covers, and platform. Two unrelated products also share the name. **AI Renamer Desktop** is a commercial app at airenamer.app. **AI Renamer CLI** is `ozgrozer/ai-renamer` on GitHub, a free GPL-3.0 Node.js tool. Both are compared below.

## Zush

<BlogToolMeta platform="Mac (macOS 15+) and Windows 10/11" website="https://zushapp.com/" label="zushapp.com">

![Zush AI rename results showing files with descriptive before and after names and green checkmarks](/images/showcase/macos/batch-rename-light.webp)

Zush is a desktop AI file renamer for Mac and Windows. You add files, it proposes names, and nothing changes until you click Rename. Any suggestion can be edited with a double-click. Ready-made Templates (Movies & TV, Remove Duplicates and others), 145+ Naming Blocks and Custom AI Blocks control the pattern. After you apply, Rename History stores the original and new name, folder and time, and Undo All restores the whole batch, listing any conflicts.

It works the moment it is installed. The free tier gives 50 AI renames on managed Zush Cloud AI, with no account, card, API key or model download, and in cloud mode it needs only 4 GB of RAM. The $48 one-time license removes the limit across Zush Cloud AI, BYOK, Ollama and LM Studio, includes all future updates, covers up to 3 devices across Mac and Windows, and has a 14-day refund window.

Zush also sorts files into folders. Use Auto or write rules such as "Group invoices by company and photos by subject"; rules can be nested. Save the setup in a Template and assign it to Monitor for new files, including folders on external drives on Mac. See the [folder sorting guide](/docs/folder-sorting) and [folder monitoring](/docs/folder-monitoring).

## AI Renamer Desktop (airenamer.app)

<BlogToolMeta platform="Mac, Windows and Linux desktop" website="https://airenamer.app/" label="airenamer.app">
  <Fragment slot="pricing">10 free cloud credits after Google sign-in; Local $19 one-time (your own key or local model); Cloud $10 per 200 credits (1 credit per renamed file)</Fragment>
</BlogToolMeta>

![AI Renamer interface (image: airenamer.app)](/images/blog/competitors/ai-renamer.webp)

AI Renamer Desktop costs $19 once for Local mode or $10 per 200 cloud credits, and gives 10 free credits when you sign in with Google. Local mode works with Ollama and LM Studio offline, or with OpenAI, Google AI Studio, OpenRouter or a custom endpoint using your own key. There are Mac builds for Apple Silicon and Intel, a Windows installer, and Linux support.

November 2026 brought two big releases:

- **v1.9.0 (November 12):** a Watch Folder beta that renames new files a few seconds after they arrive. It runs only while the app is open, leaves subfolders and existing files alone, and never overwrites a file. Renamed files appear in a History page.
- **v1.10.0 (November 15):** video, audio, HEIC, AVIF, BMP and RAW photos; Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OpenDocument and EPUB files; Name Templates built from fields such as title, date and company; Intel Mac and Linux arm64 builds.

What is still missing from its documentation is undo. The History page shows old and new names, but neither the site nor the changelog describes reverting a batch.

## AI Renamer CLI (open source)

`ozgrozer/ai-renamer` is a free GPL-3.0 command-line tool you run with `npx ai-renamer /path`. It uses Ollama by default, and also LM Studio or an OpenAI key. It renames images and, with ffmpeg installed, videos from extracted frames. Its README does not list which document types it reads, and it does not mention undo.

It fits developers who want a scriptable terminal step. For everyday file management there is no preview, no GUI and no folder watching.

## Undo and recovery

This is the clearest difference between the two desktop apps. Zush's [Rename History](/docs/undo-history) is stored on disk, so you can revert a batch days later, after restarts. Undo All flags conflicts, such as a new file now using an old name, and offers to restore under a new name. Duplicate copies moved to the Trash during AI Rename come back with Undo too.

AI Renamer keeps a History list of what was renamed but documents no way to reverse it. If 5% of a 500-file batch comes out wrong, plan to fix those by hand, or test on copies first.

## Folder monitoring

Both apps now watch folders. AI Renamer's Watch Folder is a beta that only runs while the app is open and only looks at the top level of a folder.

Zush's Monitor applies a full Template to new files: naming pattern, AI mode, duplicate handling and optional folder sorting. On Mac it also watches external drives and picks up again after you reconnect one.

## Formats

AI Renamer 1.10.0 closed most of the format gap: video, audio, RAW, HEIC, Office and EPUB are now listed. It does not publish a per-extension list for RAW cameras or video containers, and design files (PSD, Sketch, Figma) are not mentioned.

Zush publishes its full list, including 11 RAW variants, Sketch, Figma, Illustrator and Photoshop files, iWork documents, and video in MP4, MOV, MKV, AVI and WMV. For video it reads frames, speech, embedded subtitles and metadata. See the [supported file types](/docs/supported-file-types).

## Which one should you pick?

**Choose Zush if:**

- You want a persistent history and batch undo for every rename
- You want a one-time price that includes managed cloud AI, so you never manage a model or a provider bill
- You want to start without a sign-in, card or key
- You use Mac and Windows machines and want one license for up to 3 devices
- You need design files, MKV video, or duplicate handling in the same pass

**Choose AI Renamer Desktop if:**

- You run Linux
- You already run Ollama or LM Studio, or have an API key, and want the lowest one-time price ($19)
- A watcher that runs while the app is open is enough for you

**Choose the AI Renamer CLI if:**

- You want a free, scriptable terminal tool and are comfortable setting up Node.js and a local model

## Setup effort

Zush installs as a native app from a signed Mac download, the Mac App Store, Homebrew, or the Microsoft Store. Cloud mode works right away; local mode needs Ollama or LM Studio.

AI Renamer Desktop installs as a normal app, then asks you to sign in with Google. Unlimited use means buying Local and running a model yourself or adding a key.

The CLI needs Node.js, npm, a local model through Ollama or LM Studio (or an OpenAI key), and ffmpeg for video.

## FAQ

### Does AI Renamer have folder monitoring now?

Yes. Version 1.9.0 (November 12, 2026) added a Watch Folder beta. It renames new files a few seconds after they arrive, but only while the app is open, and it skips subfolders and files already in the folder. Zush's Monitor applies a full Template, including folder sorting and duplicate handling, and on Mac also watches external drives.

### Can I undo renames in AI Renamer?

AI Renamer shows a History page with old and new names, but its site and changelog do not describe undo or revert. Zush keeps a persistent Rename History, so you can undo a whole batch after a restart, with conflicts listed before anything is restored. Whichever app you use, test a new setup on copies first.

### Is AI Renamer cheaper than Zush?

AI Renamer's Local license is $19 versus Zush's $48, but they include different things. AI Renamer Local runs on your own key or a local model; cloud renames cost $10 per 200 credits. Zush's $48 includes unlimited managed cloud AI as well as BYOK and local models, all future updates, 3 devices across Mac and Windows, and a 14-day refund.

### Where are files processed in each app?

With Ollama or LM Studio, both apps keep analysis on your computer. AI Renamer's cloud credits and own-key providers, and Zush Cloud AI and BYOK, send the content needed for naming to a cloud model. AI Renamer needs a Google sign-in for its free credits; Zush needs no account at all.

### Are AI Renamer Desktop and the AI Renamer CLI from the same company?

No. The CLI is `ozgrozer/ai-renamer` on GitHub, licensed GPL-3.0. The desktop app at airenamer.app is a separate commercial product. They share a name but not a codebase, pricing or support, so check which one a review or tutorial is talking about.

### Does AI Renamer run on Linux?

Yes. AI Renamer lists Mac, Windows and Linux, and version 1.10.0 added Linux arm64 builds. Zush runs on Mac and Windows only, so if Linux is a requirement, AI Renamer or the CLI is the better fit. Its Watch Folder download buttons cover Mac and Windows; check Linux support for that feature before relying on it.

## Verdict

AI Renamer Desktop is a much closer competitor than it was a month ago: it watches folders, reads video, RAW and Office files, and runs on Linux.

Choose <BlogHighlight>Zush</BlogHighlight> for persistent batch undo, a one-time price that includes managed cloud AI, no sign-in, and design and MKV support. Choose AI Renamer for Linux or the $19 local license. Use the CLI only if you want a terminal tool.
