## Why people look for a Sparkle alternative

[Sparkle](https://makeitsparkle.co/), from Every, organizes Desktop, Downloads, Documents, and other folders on a schedule. It builds an AI Library of folders, accepts natural-language requests, and cleans up duplicates and old screenshots. For a personal Mac that just needs to stay tidy, it works.

The common reasons to look elsewhere:

- **Filenames stay the same.** Sparkle moves `IMG_2041.jpg` and `document (3).pdf` into folders but does not rename them, so they are still hard to search.
- **Mac only.** There is no Windows version.
- **Subscription.** The marketing page lists plans from $9.25 a month and the plans page showed $119 a year when checked. It is also part of the $30-a-month Every bundle.
- **The structure is the app's.** Sparkle builds its own library from patterns it detects. If you want folders per client, supplier, or month, you want to spell that out.

## 1. Zush: sorting you describe, plus real filenames

[Zush](/mac) does what Sparkle does for folders and adds what it skips: names. Turn on **Sort**, choose a destination, and write **Folder rules** in plain language, up to 2,000 characters. Leave the rules empty for Auto grouping.

- **Reads the content.** PDFs and scans, photos including RAW and HEIC, screenshots, documents, design files, videos, and audio.
- **Renames in the same batch.** A Template decides the filename, such as `2026-06-12 – Acme Supply – Invoice 10234.pdf`.
- **Uses your folders.** Reuse existing folders files into matching folders you already have.
- **Keeps going.** Assign the Template to Monitor, and new files in Downloads or a scanner folder are renamed and sorted as they arrive.
- **Can stay offline.** With LM Studio or Ollama, analysis and folder planning run on your Mac.
- **Reversible.** Nothing moves until you apply. Undo moves a file back to its original folder and name.

Sparkle is stronger for disk cleanup: duplicates, old screenshots, and app leftovers. Zush does not delete files. Folder sorting in Zush is labeled beta, and each batch creates one level of folders inside the destination. ## 2. Sortio: prompt-based sorting on three platforms

[Sortio](https://www.getsortio.com/) takes a plain-English prompt, builds a plan, renames, creates folders, and moves files, on Mac, Windows, and Linux. The free plan has unlimited rule-based sorting with up to five rules and a one-time AI trial; Pro is $14.99 a month or $99 a year with an AI allowance. See [Zush vs Sortio](/blog/zush-vs-sortio).

## 3. Hazel: precise rules, no AI

[Hazel](https://www.noodlesoft.com/) watches folders and acts on conditions you write: name, kind, date, or matched text. It can move, tag, rename by pattern, and run scripts. It is $42 and very reliable once set up, but it does not understand what a photo or scan shows. See the [Hazel alternative](/hazel-alternative) page.

## 4. Floxtop: offline sorting on Apple Silicon

[Floxtop](https://floxtop.com/) classifies files on device and suggests destination folders for review. Its official pricing listed a free tier for 100 files a month and a $19.99 one-time Pro license. It requires macOS 15 or later on Apple Silicon.

## 5. Files Magic AI: organizer plus cleaner

[Files Magic AI](https://1dot.ai/files-magic-ai) proposes a folder structure from filenames, renames files with an on-device model, and cleans duplicates and caches. It costs $4.99 a month, $29 a year, or $49.99 lifetime. See [Zush vs Files Magic AI](/blog/zush-vs-files-magic-ai).

## How to choose

- You want to decide the folder logic and get descriptive filenames: **Zush**.
- You also use Windows: **Zush** or **Sortio**.
- You need Linux: **Sortio**.
- Your files follow exact patterns and you like scripting: **Hazel**.
- You mainly want storage cleanup: stay with **Sparkle** or try **Files Magic AI**.

## FAQ

### What is the best Sparkle alternative for Mac?

Zush is the closest Sparkle alternative when you want control. It sorts files into folders by content following your plain-language rules or Auto, renames each file descriptively, works on Mac and Windows, and costs $48 one-time or $10 a month.

### Does Sparkle rename files?

No. Sparkle organizes files into folders and cleans up storage, but it does not give files new descriptive names. Zush does both in one reviewed batch.

### Is there a free Sparkle alternative?

Zush includes 50 free AI renames with folder sorting. Floxtop has a free tier for 100 files a month, Sortio has free rule-based sorting, and the open-source AI File Sorter is free.

### Can I use Zush and Sparkle together?

Yes, but let only one of them move files in a given folder. For example, use Zush with Monitor on Downloads and a scanner folder, and keep Sparkle for storage cleanup elsewhere.
