Zush has a free starter tier for testing the workflow and paid PRO plans for ongoing use. Check the [pricing section](/#pricing) for the current checkout options before purchasing.

## Free tier

The free tier gives you 50 AI renames shared across [Zush Cloud AI](/docs/zush-cloud-ai), [BYOK](/docs/byok), [LM Studio](/docs/lm-studio), and [Ollama](/docs/offline-ai). [Install the app](/docs/install-update) and use the free renames on a small batch that represents your normal work: screenshots, PDFs, photos, documents, design files, audio, or video.

Templates, Naming Blocks, [Custom AI Blocks](/docs/custom-ai-blocks), and custom prompts are included free — you can build your full naming setup before upgrading.

## Previews and batch size

Free also has a separate allowance for analysis: generating and regenerating suggestions can use this allowance even before you apply 50 renames. Check the limit message in the app if you can no longer generate suggestions.

A single manual batch currently accepts up to **50 files on Free** or **1,000 files on PRO**. PRO removes the total rename limit, but the per-batch size still applies. [Quick Rename](/docs/quick-rename) has its own selection limit on Windows.

## PRO

PRO is built for ongoing file organization. Current public product copy describes two PRO options:

| Plan | Fit |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly PRO | Flexible access for ongoing AI file renaming. |
| One-Time PRO | One-time purchase for users who prefer lifetime access. |

PRO removes the 50-rename limit across every AI mode. The processing modes themselves are already available on the free tier, along with Templates, Naming Blocks, Custom AI Blocks, and custom prompts.

To upgrade, open the current [pricing section](/#pricing) and choose Monthly PRO or One-Time PRO.

## BYOK costs

BYOK lets you use your own Gemini, OpenRouter, OpenAI, or Claude provider key. Start with the [BYOK setup guide](/docs/byok). Zush does not set your provider's API pricing; your provider bills usage according to its own terms.

## Local AI costs

[Ollama](/docs/offline-ai) and [LM Studio](/docs/lm-studio) do not use cloud model calls. Local processing depends on your computer, installed models, disk space, and model performance. The [local AI overview](/docs/local-ai) compares both routes.
